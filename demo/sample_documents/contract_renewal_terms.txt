Contract Renewal Terms - Custody & Reporting Services

Provider: Meridian Trust Group
Client: Acme Capital Partners LLC
Agreement: Master Services Agreement dated 1 January 2024

Renewal terms proposed for the 1 January 2027 renewal:
- Term: twelve (12) months, renewable.
- Annual fee: $180,000, up from $150,000 (a 20% increase).
- Fee increase requires written notice at least ninety (90) days before
  renewal date.
- Auto-renewal: the agreement auto-renews for successive one-year terms unless
  either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days
  before the renewal date.
- Termination for convenience: either party may terminate for convenience with
  120 days' written notice; a termination fee of 25% of the remaining annual
  fee applies.
- Service levels: 99.9% uptime for reporting access; penalties accrue at 5%
  of the monthly fee per missed SLA hour, capped at 15% of the monthly fee.
