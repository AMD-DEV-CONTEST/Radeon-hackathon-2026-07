CONFIDENTIAL
Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

Between Acme Capital Partners LLC ("Acme") and Meridian Trust Group ("Meridian").

Effective Date: 15 June 2026
Governing Law: State of Delaware

1. Purpose. This Agreement permits the parties to exchange confidential
   information solely to evaluate a potential merger transaction.

2. Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and remains in force for
   three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in writing by either party with
   thirty (30) days' notice.

3. Confidential Information. Confidential Information includes financial
   statements, client lists, unreleased earnings, and all discussions regarding
   deal terms. Each party must protect the other's Confidential Information
   with the same care it uses for its own, and in no event less than
   reasonable care.

4. No Use. Confidential Information may only be used to evaluate the proposed
   transaction and may not be used for any competing investment activity.

5. No Solicitation. For eighteen (18) months after the Effective Date, neither
   party may solicit or hire any employee of the other party without written
   consent.

6. Return or Destruction. Upon written request, each party must return or
   destroy all Confidential Information within ten (10) business days.

7. Remedies. The parties agree that monetary damages are inadequate for breach
   and that injunctive relief is available in the Delaware Court of Chancery.
