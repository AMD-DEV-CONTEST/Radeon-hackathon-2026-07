# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for contributors
of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and consent.
- Report security concerns privately instead of publishing exploit details.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect users, contributors, or the
  integrity of the competition submission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate
misrepresentation of evidence, credential disclosure, and sustained disruption
are not permitted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject
contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies to repositories, discussions, reviews, community channels,
events, and private communication made in an official project capacity.
