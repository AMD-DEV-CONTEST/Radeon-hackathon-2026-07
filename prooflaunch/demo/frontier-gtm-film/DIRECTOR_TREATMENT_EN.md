## Creative north star

The film is a product keynote, not a montage. Its single causal sentence is:

> One approved product truth becomes three platform-native campaigns, remains inspectable through every transformation, runs on Radeon, and stops at the human release boundary.

The audience must understand the product before admiring the craft. Every movement either reveals state, preserves continuity, or proves a claim. There are no decorative 3D objects, generic particles, fake terminals, or invented performance numbers.

## Direction system

- Editorial rhythm: confident, spacious, evidence-led. Average shot length 7–12 seconds; only the opening and channel split accelerate.
- Camera grammar: locked wide shot for orientation; deliberate push-in for decisions; lateral move for platform divergence; no arbitrary orbiting.
- Product continuity: the cursor never teleports across an operation. Click, resulting state, and output stay in the same spatial sequence.
- UI legibility: 1920×1080 delivery, source captures up to 3840×2160, minimum important text size 32 px in the final frame.
- Motion hierarchy: one primary action per shot. Background movement is capped at 8% scale drift and never competes with the cursor.
- Color grammar: neutral product canvas; blue for operator action; violet/green/orange only for LinkedIn/YouTube/Search ownership.
- Subtitle safe area: two lines maximum, 42 characters per line target, 8% bottom inset, opaque 82% black plate when placed over UI.
- Sound grammar: restrained pulse for system state, tactile click transients for real actions, distinct channel motifs, silence before the Radeon proof and final verdict.

## English voiceover

What if a launch system could move fast without losing the evidence behind every decision?

This is Frontier GTM Studio. One approved product truth enters the workspace. The system turns that truth into platform-native campaign kits, while keeping every copy decision, visual source, placement and release gate inspectable.

We begin with a real operating job: Technical Proof. The operator selects the recipe directly in the product. No hidden jump. No pre-recorded result substituted for the workflow.

The Agent council converts the brief into a controlled production run. Twelve specialist genomes coordinate through six production nodes. Strategy, copy, image direction, film construction and review remain bound to the same run envelope.

Now the system separates one product truth into three different audience jobs.

LinkedIn opens with the verdict. Its four-by-five kit is designed for evaluation-stage proof review.

YouTube begins with participation. Its vertical kit asks the viewer to predict which claim will survive before the evidence is revealed.

Google Search begins with provenance. Its sixteen-by-nine explainer turns a buying question into an inspectable evidence path.

These are not one asset resized three times. Each surface owns its copy payload, placement identifier, creative source and final binary.

Inside the real product interface, each kit can be inspected independently. The image is downloadable. The film is playable. The result remains connected to its source and intended surface.

The three films contain eighteen independently sourced storyboard shots. Six for LinkedIn. Six for YouTube. Six for Search. Across the final package, binary reuse, source reuse and placement reuse are all zero.

[RADEON HOST CAPTURE — narration must be completed only after observation.] Here, the same project is running on an AMD Radeon GPU through ROCm. The terminal shows the detected Radeon device, HIP runtime, available memory and the exact command used for this run. Live utilization remains visible until the program completes. The outputs are then verified as real media. LinkedIn is four-by-five at eight seconds. YouTube is nine-by-sixteen at twelve seconds. Search is sixteen-by-nine at ten seconds. Each film contains an H.264 video stream and AAC stereo audio, and each file has a unique content hash.

Finally, the system makes its boundary explicit. Twelve mechanical quality gates have passed. Human craft preference, rights clearance and live-market effect are not automated claims. They remain named-human release decisions.

Frontier GTM Studio makes the campaign—and keeps the evidence.
