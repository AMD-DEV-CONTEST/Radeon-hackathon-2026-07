# ProofLaunch Cortex-X V10 execution order

The 1,500 TODOs are atomic evidence units, not 1,500 independent features.
They remain governed by their exact source wording in
`CORTEX_X_V10_1500_TODO.md`. This document defines the dependency order that
prevents later work from laundering assumptions made earlier.

## Closure rule

Every base item needs distinct D/A/I/E evidence pointers. Every item from 1201
through 1500 also needs its declared T, P, or X pointer. A locator, lowercase
SHA-256, evidence class, and conclusion are mandatory. A falsified experiment
may close, but its mechanism cannot enter the product path.

## W0 — rules, rights, safety, Radeon

Workstreams 01, 09, 22, and 23 freeze the competition contract, data rights,
truth/safety boundaries, and the official Radeon/ROCm runtime. The execution
contract is multi-GPU-first: at least two physical GPUs plus RCCL are required
for direct multi-GPU evidence, while one GPU is only a non-promotable
equivalence/debugging baseline. No training, benchmark certification, or
product claim can outrun this wave.

## W1 — reuse, research, data, evaluation

Workstreams 02, 03, 04, 10, 11, and 21 establish upstream reuse decisions,
academic hypotheses, the five-layer distillation model, rights-audited agency
corpus, decontamination, frozen cases, baselines, and human review protocols.

## W2 — typed control plane

Workstreams 05, 06, 07, 08, 12, and 18 establish campaign state, one
authoritative Supervisor, trajectory credit, brand memory, self-evolution
curriculum, and the bounded 4A capability surface.

## W3 — model training

Workstreams 13–17 remain dependency-sequential but execute inside each stage
with multi-GPU DDP after memory-fit preflight, or FSDP2 only after equivalence
validation as the memory fallback: multimodal continued pretraining,
curriculum SFT/direct feedback, on-policy distillation, multi-objective RL,
then sparse adapter consolidation. Every stage freezes world size, global
batch and revisions, and requires per-rank receipts, a retention gate, atomic
checkpointing, and rollback.

## W4 — 4K media

Workstreams 19 and 20 implement native working resolution plus truthful 4K
mastering, protected product/logo layers, post-codec identity, OCR, color, and
region-time quality gates. “Native 4K” is forbidden unless the generator
actually produced that resolution.

## W5–W8 — twelve falsifiable breakthroughs

- W5: causal market twin, Campaign Genome, and task-focused memory.
- W6: action-conditioned planning, dynamic bounded agency, sparse adapters,
  and outcome-aware test-time compute.
- W7: proof-carrying compiler, human-calibrated 4A Taste, and local
  region-time reward/repair.
- W8: randomized experiment autopilot and the versioned Campaign Operating
  Space.

Each bet is evaluated against the five frozen baselines on technical, product,
and effect axes. All twelve must produce evidence and at least three must pass
all three axes before they can be called breakthroughs.

## W9 — submission

Workstream 24 executes only after upstream gates. It packages exact source,
README, PDF, poster/PPT, 3–5 minute video, licensed data/model inventory,
Radeon/ROCm traces, negative results, blind-review evidence, and the official
fork/PR. Day 6 is submission-only; it is not a recovery day for missing
training or evidence.

## Current truth

The local control plane and V10 contracts are executable. The target Radeon
kernel, V10 corpus, six-stage training run, real 4K campaign outputs, five-person
blind review, demo video, and official PR are not yet evidenced. They must
remain blocked in the submission gate until their artifacts exist and hash
verification passes.
