## Same-brief PK protocol

- **Brief:** One frozen, rights-cleared brief and evidence bundle per comparison.
- **Business Outcome Boundary:** Publication, spend, lift and revenue are measured later and cannot retroactively replace the production-quality verdict.
- **Comparison:** Candidate and rights-cleared peer/reference output are rendered in the same channel format with creator, provider and model identity hidden.
- **Dimensions:** The seven lane-specific quality axes plus a forced overall preference and critical-defect checklist.
- **Judge Boundary:** Model judges may flag defects and explain disagreement but cannot select or release a winner.
- **Promotion Threshold:** Zero critical defects, at least 75% candidate preference, Wilson lower bound above 0.50, at least 95% directly publishable votes, no more than 5% major-rework votes, and no quality dimension with a statistically credible regression.
- **Reviewers:** At least five qualified reviewers per lane; strategy/measurement lanes require relevant operators and creative lanes require senior craft reviewers.

## Per-lane quality axes

- **Integrated GTM strategy:** diagnostic depth, strategic choice, commercial logic, creative platform, channel coherence, evidence discipline, operator usability.
- **Organic social copy:** hook strength, originality, platform nativeness, brand voice, proof integration, CTA quality, edit readiness.
- **Landing-page draft:** message hierarchy, clarity, proof architecture, objection handling, conversion flow, brand distinctiveness, production polish.
- **Lifecycle email:** subject strength, reader relevance, narrative progression, proof integration, voice, CTA quality, send readiness.
- **Visual campaign system:** art direction, originality, brand fit, hierarchy, cross-format coherence, artifact defects, production polish.
- **Campaign video:** idea, first-three-second hook, shot craft, motion continuity, sound-picture coherence, brand fit, delivery polish.
- **Voice and audio master:** semantic accuracy, performance, naturalness, mix, brand voice, accessibility, delivery polish.
- **Content schedule:** sequence logic, channel cadence, format fit, resource realism, learning cadence, fatigue control, operator usability.
- **SEO execution:** intent coverage, information gain, SERP usefulness, technical readiness, internal-link logic, claim accuracy, conversion relevance.
- **GEO / answer visibility:** answer completeness, citation readiness, entity clarity, retrievability, question coverage, claim accuracy, brand inclusion.
- **Advertising plan:** audience-strategy fit, creative-route fit, budget logic, test design, incrementality design, risk control, operator clarity.
- **Analytics and experiment plan:** metric validity, identity-safe measurement, causal design, stopping logic, revenue linkage, diagnostic usefulness, operator clarity.
- **Graph / Loop / Harness:** task completion, recovery quality, tool discipline, traceability, approval integrity, failure containment, operator control.
- **Cross-device memory continuity:** retrieval relevance, conflict handling, temporal validity, privacy boundary, human control, cross-device consistency, non-mutation guarantee.
