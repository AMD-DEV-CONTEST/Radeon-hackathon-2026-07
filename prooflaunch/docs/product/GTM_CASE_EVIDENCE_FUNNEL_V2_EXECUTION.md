# GTM Case Evidence Funnel V2 — Execution Status

## Outcome

The 81-subvertical, 200→40→10→5 requirement is now an executable, fail-closed evidence system rather than a narrative target.

- 81 frozen subvertical IDs.
- Every subvertical now has a substantive definition, three inclusion rules, three exclusion rules, sibling boundaries, and exclusive-primary classification. New subverticals require a versioned named proposal and inherit the full 200/40/10/5 obligation.
- 200 structured case episodes per subvertical.
- 40 mechanism deep audits, 10 outcome cross-verifications, and 5 execution blueprints per subvertical.
- Global targets: 16,200 / 3,240 / 810 / 405.
- Six independent marginal quotas inside every 200-case set: company scale, lifecycle, budget, recency, outcome type, and market.
- Cross-platform mirrors count once through a stable episode key.
- Every candidate records six separate cost components: paid media, personnel, tools, creators, channel fees, and production. Unknown cost remains explicit, and non-media labor cannot be silently treated as free.
- Any missing count or marginal quota produces `coverage_gap`; weak, duplicate, undisclosed-budget, or unreviewed material cannot backfill it.
