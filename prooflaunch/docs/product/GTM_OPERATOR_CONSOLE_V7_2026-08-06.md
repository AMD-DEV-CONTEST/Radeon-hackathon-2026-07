# ProofLaunch GTM Operator Console V7 — 4A Candidate Production System

## Outcome

The judge-facing operator console now runs one coherent production sequence:

1. Brief — seal the closed context and approved product truth.
2. Strategy — choose one audience decision, one category default to reject and one non-consensus bet.
3. Concept — produce three materially distinct territories with an inspectable kill rule.
4. Production — generate platform-native copy, three PNG formats and one playable MP4.
5. Audit — mechanically verify truth boundary, territory distinction, platform nativeness, cross-modal continuity and binary completeness.
6. Release — remain blocked until a named human completes craft, rights and release review.

The console does not label generated work as proven 4A quality. It labels the package **4A candidate**, records the mechanical gates that passed, and exposes the remaining human blind-review and live-market-effect requirements.

## Differentiated production recipes

The five one-click recipes now select different strategy and creative playbooks rather than changing only their titles:

- Evidence-led category launch — the proof mechanism becomes the recognizable campaign property.
- Technical rejection test — a falsifiable decision rule leads and surviving evidence carries the argument.
- Founder conviction — the non-obvious operating decision, not founder biography, becomes the narrative spine.
- B2C participation ritual — one useful visible behavior becomes the media, proof and relay mechanic.
- Category reframe — replace the incumbent comparison frame before competing on features.

Each playbook changes the strategic diagnosis, campaign platform, hook, visual metaphor, participation mechanic and native surface copy. B2B produces LinkedIn, YouTube and search-answer units; B2C produces Instagram, TikTok and search-answer units.

## Real output contract

- Authenticated and idempotent `POST /v1/realtime-preset-campaigns` compiles the selected recipe.
- The response includes strategy, three territories, a jury scorecard, channel-native copy, image system, film system, channel portfolio, six-stage production receipt, quality-control receipt and final audit.
- Three real PNGs and one playable H.264 MP4 are generated and served through tenant-authorized content-addressed URLs.
- The observed renderer is disclosed as Pillow + ImageIO + FFmpeg. Image/video foundation-model inference is not claimed on this preset path.
- The browser renders only server-returned work, exposes asset hashes and downloads, and never substitutes fake client-side campaign results.
- Publish, send and spend remain unavailable until the independent named-human gate.

## Interface repair and information architecture

- Repaired production delivery for minified CSS, minified JavaScript, evaluator files and allowlisted visual assets. The production server now displays the designed console instead of unstyled HTML.
- Reduced the primary judge path to recipe selection, seven visible brief fields, one primary generation action and four result tabs.
- Advanced campaign intelligence remains available in one progressive-disclosure section.
- Added a six-stage progress rail, dynamic run inspector, manifest download and explicit approval route.
- Result tabs separate overview, platform copy, rendered media and audit evidence without hiding the end-to-end relationship.
