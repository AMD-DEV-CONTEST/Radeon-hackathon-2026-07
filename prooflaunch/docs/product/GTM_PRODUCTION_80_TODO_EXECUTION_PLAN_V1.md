# ProofLaunch GTM production 80-TODO execution plan V1

As of: 2026-08-05 (America/New_York)  
North Star: qualified Brief to first publishable asset time × publishable rate × qualified conversion rate. Asset volume is never a substitute for outcome quality.

## Status legend and four-hour freeze

- `M` — mechanically implemented and covered by the current local production path.
- `B` — runtime exists but a named human or real-world evidence gate is intentionally open.
- `Q` — queued after the submission freeze; it may have partial repository components but is not credited here.
- `H` — requires real named-human or market execution and cannot be synthesized by code.

The four-hour submission freeze is limited to regression, evidence regeneration, final locks, documentation and integrity checks. It cannot truthfully manufacture editorial votes, named-product baselines, publication receipts, lift or revenue.

1. **T001 · M · GTM North Star** — optimize time-to-first-publishable × publishable rate × qualified conversion; reject volume proxies.
2. **T002 · M · Three truth states** — keep `mechanically_ready`, `human_publishable`, and `production_proven` separate in API, UI and receipts.
3. **T003 · M · Four gates** — G0 contract, G1 professional editor, G2 same-Brief blind preference, G3 observed distribution effect.
4. **T004 · M · Direct publishability** — target ≥90%; structural edits fail the gate.

6. **T006 · M · Blocking defects** — false claims, rights risk, platform mismatch, wrong CTA, identity drift or unreadable captions block the pack.
7. **T007 · M · Outcome tree** — select qualified action, lead, registration, activation, revenue or retention before execution.
