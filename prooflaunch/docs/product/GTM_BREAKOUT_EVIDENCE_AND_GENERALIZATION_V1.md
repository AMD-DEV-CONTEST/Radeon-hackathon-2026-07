# GTM Breakout Evidence and Generalization V1

## Purpose

This contract turns 2020-01-01 through 2026-08-04 creative, growth, and research evidence into bounded generation rules for English-priority B2B and B2C GTM work. It is not a campaign-copy library and does not treat award recognition, followers, views, pipeline, or ARR as interchangeable outcomes.

## Evidence boundary

- Award-body records can validate the work, award, reported result, and described mechanism. They do not by themselves prove zero spend or causal revenue lift.
- Company and founder growth metrics remain primary self-reports unless an independent audited source is attached.
- “Mostly organic” is not “zero spend.” Affiliate payouts, founder time, production, partnerships, sponsorships, owned inventory, and event media must remain visible.
- No case in the current ledger has primary verified zero-paid-media status. The product may design a zero-paid launch route, but cannot cite a historical guarantee.
- Source expression, creator voice, identities, comments, and campaign copy cannot be reused. Only abstract mechanisms and falsifiable measurement designs may transfer.

- Year distribution: 10 / 11 / 6 / 9 / 25 / 24 / 25 cases from 2020 through 2026.

- Six P0 and sixteen P1 cases prioritize industrial, manufacturing, software, banking, agriculture, medical, professional-work, B2B, and English-priority transfer gaps.

Every discovery row is `training_eligible=false` and `pending_case_level_claim_audit`. An official award listing confirms the named work and category context only; it does not establish organic distribution, zero spend, conversion, revenue, causality, or transferability. Promotion into the 24-case verified ledger remains manual and requires all five evidence tasks. The two tiers overlap, so they must not be presented as 134 unique cases.

## Breakout-team quality screen

YC and other accelerator directories are candidate and identity maps only. Accelerator membership, funding, acquisition, or an `Active` directory label contributes **zero** to the outcome-quality score; many funded companies never produce durable growth. The current YC B2B/Consumer landscape contains 3,241 unique records and is useful for company identity, industry, stage, and sampling coverage, not for declaring winners.

The first hard-outcome screen parses 501 rows from Deloitte's official 2025 North America Technology Fast 500 table (two companies share rank 500). It uses reported fiscal-year revenue growth from 2021–2024 and the program's operating-revenue eligibility floor. The official rank is the only candidate-stage priority input:

- P0: ranks 1–50, 50 teams.
- P1: ranks 51–150, 100 teams.
- P2: ranks 151–500, 351 teams.
- YC, accelerator, funding, and directory-status quality weights: 0.

Fast 500 inclusion still does **not** prove a viral campaign, recent 3–6 month momentum, an organic channel, product quality, retention, or GTM causality. All 501 candidates remain `breakout_team_eligible=false`. Promotion requires current operating proof, a recent relative breakout, an observable channel mechanism, retention or repeatability evidence, complete paid/organic/resource scope, and human review.

The business-model gate then separates B2B and B2C. B2B requires windowed monetized growth, renewal/NRR or an equivalent cohort signal, distribution efficiency, repetition across at least three cohorts or cycles, and inspectable last-mile assets tied to activation and revenue. B2C requires windowed active-user/customer/revenue growth, D30/D90 or repeat-purchase evidence, a paid-versus-organic split, repetition across at least three cohorts/content cycles/markets, and creative tied to activation, retention, and monetization. Funding pedigree, vanity reach, a single viral post, undisclosed paid growth, an unbounded founder claim, or inactive operation blocks promotion.

Zero budget is **not** a mandatory winner definition. The resource gate evaluates a comparable 6–12 month cold-start window and accepts zero-paid, low-budget, capital-efficient, mixed-budget, and mature-scale evidence as separate tiers. The transferable requirement is that resources are disclosed and connected to activation, retention, monetization, and repeatability. A low-budget team with a strong closed-loop GTM system can outrank a zero-paid team with weak retention or no revenue bridge.

The expanded quality seed contains 34 teams with explicit evidence gaps rather than treating a large directory as a winner list:

- **27 mechanism-distillation-ready teams:** the previous 15 plus Solberg Manufacturing, Reposit Power, Wingtra, Viessmann, InSinkErator, Rever, Smash My Trash, FBi Buildings, 2030 Builders, Pure Bookkeeping, Strategic IC, and Beard & Blade Wholesale.
- **Seven outcome seeds only:** Gamma, Lovable, Vanta, Duolingo, Cursor, Buffer, and Canva. Their outcomes justify further audit, but missing channel attribution, retention, recency, independent corroboration, or resource scope blocks mechanism promotion.
- Four cases report a zero-paid cold-start or operating path; other strong cases use low, mixed, or mature budgets. Every zero-paid statement remains labeled as company/founder/platform reporting unless independently audited.

All 34 remain `breakout_team_fully_promoted=false`, `zero_budget_proof=false`, and `copy_training_eligible=false`. The purpose is to distill bounded causal hypotheses, never company copy or an unsupported promise.

Twenty-one executable top-level blueprints are separately sealed. The six new non-software B2B mechanisms are technical specification to qualified demand, channel-partner demand and execution, B2B self-service reorder, long-cycle proof-assisted selling, professional authority to recurring contract, and paid-dependence to owned-intent reset. They join the original fifteen mechanisms. Every blueprint includes B2B and B2C adaptations, platform translations, the attention-to-revenue chain, instrumentation, kill rules, resource fit, and evidence limits.

The traditional-industry expansion deliberately separates three evidence classes. Solberg supplies a recent six-month industrial-commerce control. Reposit Power, 2030 Builders, Pure Bookkeeping, Rever, and Smash My Trash provide bounded operating-change cases with explicit revenue or sales bridges. Wingtra, Viessmann, InSinkErator, FBi Buildings, Strategic IC, and Beard & Blade preserve useful historical mechanisms but carry a missing or partial recency flag. Vendor and consultancy case studies remain labeled as such; none is treated as independently audited causal proof.

The blueprints are routed—not averaged—across all 140 business-model × market × vertical cells and all 168 business-model × market × platform lanes. Each cell has separate pre-PMF, launch, growth, and mature routes plus zero-paid, low-budget, mixed-budget, and mature-scale resource logic. Each platform lane preserves its public/authorized access contract and requires measurement through qualified attention, owned/activation events, retention or pipeline, conversion/revenue, and resource cost. The selector chooses one primary mechanism and at most one reinforcing mechanism after diagnosis.

## Eight mechanisms permitted for generation

1. **Product performs the claim.** The campaign contains an inspectable proof object made by the product.
2. **Medium is the demonstration.** The idea lives inside the audience's native work object, sensory experience, or operational touchpoint.
3. **Participation creates distribution.** Each voluntary contribution creates utility or evidence even if nobody else sees it.
4. **Translate into audience language.** Strategy uses the segment's decision tension, proof standard, and real vocabulary.
5. **Curiosity resolves into truth.** A benign, bounded question resolves through stronger proof and never through impersonation or falsehood.
6. **Zero audience is not zero distribution.** Low follower count does not disqualify a test, but a credible first-hundred qualified-viewer route is mandatory.
7. **Human specificity beats AI volume.** Human observation, judgment, effort, accountability, and field evidence are required quality signals.
8. **Attention requires a revenue bridge.** Every asset names the next proof unit, activation event, retention signal, conversion event, and kill rule.

Each mechanism cites evidence-labeled cases, contains B2B and B2C adaptations, preserves failure conditions, and is prohibited from claiming an unsupported or unaudited zero-paid-media precedent.

Every contract defines buyer/user, decision tension, proof language, native voice, and forbidden shortcuts. Copy that can move unchanged to an unrelated industry fails.

The 140-cell organic-signal standard now also carries four or more required subverticals per cell. The `industrial_climate` cell explicitly includes manufacturing, industrial equipment, engineering/construction, commercial real estate, and energy/climate. The 140 cells and 168 platform-market lanes remain targets; they do not imply live evidence coverage.

## Release-candidate proof

Two clean-room candidates demonstrate the contract:

- **B2B / Patchline Replay / The Silent Handoff:** a redacted six-file bug packet, clean-machine replay, and RUNNABLE/BLOCKED result make the product and documentation claim inspectable.
- **B2C / Twenty Focus / Twenty-Minute Receipt:** a local-only, no-streak before-and-after artifact delivers private value before account creation and avoids therapeutic or life-transformation claims.

Each candidate has 12 GTM decision roles, 13 native release surfaces, a seven-day flight, human-gated learning, and zero automatic publish/spend/outreach authority. Social-copy pairwise Jaccard maxima are below 0.38, with unique hooks and zero defensive boilerplate hits.

These are mechanically ready examples. They do not yet prove named-product superiority, human preference, publication, organic breakout, retention, conversion, revenue, or 4A parity.
