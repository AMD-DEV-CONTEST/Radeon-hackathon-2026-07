# ProofLaunch GTM Anti-Commodity Intelligence Upgrade Plan V1

## Objective

Upgrade ProofLaunch from a mechanism-routed content agent into an adaptive GTM operating system that can diagnose any B2B or B2C business, discover a defensible growth angle, produce platform-native work with human-grade judgment, connect attention to revenue, and learn only from attributable outcomes and approved human decisions.

The product is not accepted because it can generate many assets. It is accepted only when it repeatedly produces decision-changing work that is distinctive, truthful, native to the platform, commercially coherent, operationally deliverable, and measurably better than the frozen comparator.

## Non-negotiable release gates

- No universal growth formula: industry, buyer, maturity, budget, geography, channel state, proof inventory, and fulfillment capacity must change the plan.
- No AI-volume advantage claim: output volume is a cost metric, never the quality metric.
- No copied voice or campaign expression: cases contribute mechanisms, constraints, and test designs only.
- No attention-only success: every asset must connect to an owned signal, activation, pipeline or purchase, retention, and resource cost.
- No automatic publishing, spending, outreach, or high-risk action without explicit authorization.
- No learning from unapproved model output: persistent strategy memory accepts verified outcomes and human-approved judgments only.

## 80 execution TODOs

### A. Truth, outcome, and quality contract — P0

- [ ] **T01 — Replace “content quality” with a decision-change contract.** Define the exact buyer belief, behavior, or internal decision each asset must change. Accept only when the before/after belief and observable next action are specified.
- [ ] **T02 — Create a six-axis quality function.** Score strategic fit, original insight, proof density, platform nativeness, brand distinctiveness, and commercial bridge separately; a high average cannot hide a zero on truth or proof.
- [ ] **T03 — Separate attention, trust, activation, revenue, and retention.** Prohibit impressions, views, followers, MQLs, pipeline, revenue, and retained revenue from being reported as equivalent outcomes.
- [ ] **T04 — Install a claim-evidence ledger.** Bind every numerical, comparative, technical, customer, or market claim to source, date, scope, confidence, license, and expiry; unsupported claims block release.
- [ ] **T05 — Add resource truth.** Measure cash, media, tooling, creator payments, partner economics, employee time, founder time, production cost, and fulfillment capacity; “organic” cannot conceal labor or affiliate cost.
- [ ] **T06 — Define lifecycle-specific success.** Pre-PMF optimizes learning and qualified activation; launch optimizes proof and first repeatable demand; growth optimizes retained economics; mature stages optimize expansion and portfolio efficiency.
- [ ] **T07 — Add negative-outcome accounting.** Record unsubscribes, hides, negative comments, brand confusion, sales objections, returns, low-quality leads, partner rejection, support load, and margin erosion alongside positive metrics.
- [ ] **T08 — Freeze the benchmark protocol.** Require identical brief, constraints, inputs, asset specifications, operator conditions, and source-hidden human review before claiming superiority over a named product or prior version.

### B. Industry, audience, and market ontology — P0

- [ ] **T09 — Expand B2B from broad verticals into buying systems.** Cover manufacturing, industrial equipment, chemicals, construction, engineering, real estate, logistics, wholesale, professional services, healthcare, finance, software, and public-sector selling with distinct procurement logic.
- [ ] **T10 — Expand B2C into occasion systems.** Cover mobile apps, AI tools, finance, health, education, gaming, travel, food, beauty, fashion, home, local services, marketplaces, subscriptions, and durable goods through occasion, identity, anxiety, and repeat behavior.
- [ ] **T11 — Model company stage independently from company size.** Distinguish idea, pre-PMF, founder-led sales, first repeatable channel, scaling, category leadership, stagnation, turnaround, and international expansion.
- [ ] **T12 — Build a buying-committee graph for every B2B route.** Represent user, champion, economic buyer, technical approver, procurement, legal, finance, operations, partner, detractor, and executive sponsor with separate proof requirements.
- [ ] **T13 — Build a household and social-influence graph for B2C.** Represent user, purchaser, gift giver, parent, partner, peer group, creator, reviewer, community, and post-purchase advocate.
- [ ] **T14 — Create an audience-language asset, not a persona paragraph.** Store real decision vocabulary, taboo phrases, status signals, anxieties, objections, proof standards, humor boundaries, reading level, and platform dialect by segment.
- [ ] **T15 — Add geography as a causal variable.** Separate US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong, South Africa, and relevant European English audiences by regulation, culture, media behavior, buying power, and channel norms.
- [ ] **T16 — Add business-constraint diagnosis.** Before strategy generation, score product readiness, supply or delivery capacity, gross margin, sales capacity, proof inventory, brand permission, legal constraints, and measurement maturity; block plans the business cannot fulfill.

### C. Live signal acquisition and evidence intelligence — P0

- [ ] **T17 — Replace the static case pool with a rolling 3/6/12-month signal window.** Keep historical mechanisms as priors but prevent them from overriding recent category and platform evidence.
- [ ] **T18 — Enforce sector sampling quotas.** For every priority subvertical, require breakout, failed, mature-control, low-budget, partner-led, and retention-led examples so the model learns contrast rather than survivor stories only.
- [ ] **T19 — Build cross-platform public-signal connectors.** Ingest permitted public signals from LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, X, Facebook, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon, podcasts, newsletters, search, review sites, app stores, and trade publications through authorized or policy-compliant access.
- [ ] **T20 — Capture the full public artifact context.** Preserve post or page, media metadata, author identity class, publication time, comments, replies, edits, visible engagement, destination, call to action, and later follow-up—not the caption alone.
- [ ] **T21 — Build a denominator-aware breakout detector.** Compare velocity against account baseline, category baseline, audience size, format, region, season, and posting cadence; never call a large account’s ordinary reach a breakout.
- [ ] **T22 — Add buyer-quality signals.** Separate practitioner questions, buying intent, implementation discussion, peer recommendation, complaints, entertainment response, bot-like activity, and low-value engagement.
- [ ] **T23 — Add outcome corroboration tasks.** Join public creative signals to company filings, official customer evidence, hiring, distribution, app rankings, web demand, reviews, product usage, partner data, or supplied CRM and commerce outcomes when authorized.
- [ ] **T24 — Create evidence expiry and refresh jobs.** Platform rules, pricing, product capability, market growth, and recent examples expire automatically; stale evidence can inform history but cannot authorize current execution.

### D. Adaptive strategy intelligence — P0

- [ ] **T25 — Build a strategy diagnosis compiler.** Convert business state, audience graph, category tension, proof assets, competitive behavior, resources, and channel evidence into explicit strategic choices and rejected alternatives.
- [ ] **T26 — Add category-entry-point selection.** Identify the few situations in which the buyer should remember the brand and rank them by frequency, value, competitive crowding, product truth, and ability to demonstrate.
- [ ] **T27 — Create a category tension and reframing engine.** Find the outdated assumption, hidden tradeoff, underserved job, mispriced risk, or cultural contradiction the brand can truthfully own.
- [ ] **T28 — Add “why now” synthesis.** Combine regulatory, technological, economic, social, platform, and buyer-behavior changes into a dated urgency argument; reject timeless generic urgency.
- [ ] **T29 — Build a strategic wedge portfolio.** Generate education, proof, community, utility, entertainment, controversy-with-evidence, partnership, product-led, and service-led wedges, then rank by fit rather than defaulting to content marketing.
- [ ] **T30 — Add channel concentration logic.** Select one primary and one reinforcing channel from audience presence, format advantage, proof compatibility, time to signal, compounding potential, resource cost, and fulfillment capacity.
- [ ] **T31 — Add zero/low-budget asymmetry discovery.** Search for public utilities, open data, calculators, diagnostics, templates, challenges, live teardowns, partnerships, customer artifacts, and product-carried distribution that competitors cannot cheaply imitate.
- [ ] **T32 — Build a legal “contrarian growth” library.** Encode pattern interruption, radical usefulness, public proof, category jiu-jitsu, transparent tradeoffs, unexpected collaborations, live problem solving, and community co-creation while blocking deception, fake consensus, impersonation, harassment, and platform abuse.

### E. Creative direction, copy, and narrative intelligence — P0

- [ ] **T33 — Create an original-insight requirement.** Every hero asset must contain at least one first-party observation, experiment, dataset, test, tool, operator judgment, or demonstrable product behavior unavailable from generic model recall.
- [ ] **T34 — Build a creative-territory generator.** Produce at least five materially different territories—documentary truth, expert authority, challenger humor, utility, emotional story, cultural participation, or product spectacle—before choosing execution.
- [ ] **T35 — Add a narrative-architecture selector.** Choose problem-solution, mystery-proof, transformation, tension-release, myth-test, founder-confession, customer quest, live experiment, or world-building based on audience and platform rather than template preference.
- [ ] **T36 — Build platform-specific writing rooms.** Maintain separate standards for LinkedIn operator writing, Reddit field answers, X compression, newsletter intimacy, YouTube narrative, TikTok hooks and comments, Instagram visual captions, search decision pages, and executive POV.
- [ ] **T37 — Add B2B role-specific copy transformation.** The same proposition must change for engineer, operator, CFO, procurement, executive, partner, and end user while preserving one strategic spine and avoiding synonymized duplicates.
- [ ] **T38 — Add B2C identity-and-occasion transformation.** Change hook, vocabulary, proof, emotion, objection, and CTA by use occasion, identity, sophistication, price sensitivity, and lifecycle state.
- [ ] **T39 — Install anti-generic language checks.** Penalize interchangeable claims, abstract superlatives, fake urgency, AI cadence, unearned certainty, stacked adjectives, generic CTAs, and copy that survives a competitor-name substitution.
- [ ] **T40 — Add memorability and brand-code design.** Require a distinctive phrase, visual behavior, recurring character, sonic cue, demonstration structure, or interaction ritual that compounds recognition without repeating identical content.

### F. Multimodal production and proof objects — P0/P1

- [ ] **T41 — Make the proof object the production nucleus.** Begin image, video, audio, article, landing page, demo, and sales enablement from a real test, workflow, transformation, dataset, or customer decision—not from a mood board.
- [ ] **T42 — Build shot- and evidence-level video planning.** Specify hook frame, visual question, proof sequence, human presence, product continuity, sound design, subtitles, pace, reveal, CTA, and verifiable claims before generation.
- [ ] **T43 — Add continuity and physics gates.** Test identity, product geometry, logos, hands, text, material behavior, lighting, motion, lip sync, audio-video alignment, and temporal consistency before a video is reviewable.
- [ ] **T44 — Add visual distinctiveness tests.** Compare color, composition, subject, camera grammar, typography, visual metaphor, and frame similarity against the brand archive and competitor set; reject attractive but interchangeable output.
- [ ] **T45 — Build voice and sonic-brand control.** Select voice, pacing, emotional register, accent fit, pronunciation, music role, sonic motif, silence, and mixing targets by audience and platform; preserve disclosure and usage rights.
- [ ] **T46 — Create modular master assets without clone outputs.** Share evidence and strategic spine while forcing native recomposition for long video, short video, image, carousel, article, landing page, email, sales deck, and partner kit.
- [ ] **T47 — Add human craft checkpoints.** Require explicit director, editor, copy chief, domain expert, brand, legal, and conversion reviews only where their judgment changes the work; record decisions as structured annotations.
- [ ] **T48 — Build production fallback ladders.** For every model or GPU dependency, define flagship, alternate cloud, local, deterministic template, and human-edit routes with quality deltas; infrastructure failure cannot silently reduce truth or brand standards.

### G. Platform-native distribution and demand capture — P1

- [ ] **T49 — Build a platform-job contract.** Define whether each channel performs discovery, trust, search, proof, community, capture, conversion, retention, support, or amplification; reject identical objectives across all channels.
- [ ] **T50 — Add non-follower discovery design.** Require a first-hundred qualified-viewer route based on search intent, niche communities, collaborators, customers, employees, partners, live participation, or utility distribution instead of follower assumptions.
- [ ] **T51 — Turn comments into a research loop.** Classify questions, objections, corrections, jokes, use cases, peer answers, and purchase signals; generate response and next-asset briefs without fabricating community consensus.
- [ ] **T52 — Build search–social–GEO continuity.** Map conversational queries to social proof, first-party evidence, crawlable decision pages, structured data, answer-engine citations, branded search, and owned conversion without mass doorway pages.
- [ ] **T53 — Build partner and creator suitability matching.** Score audience overlap, real expertise, format ability, reputation, disclosure fit, safety, prior brand saturation, and downstream buyer quality—not follower count alone.
- [ ] **T54 — Create launch, always-on, and moment modes.** Distinguish planned category building, recurring proof franchises, real-time cultural participation, product launches, crisis response, and seasonal opportunities with different approval SLAs.
- [ ] **T55 — Add organic-to-paid promotion rules.** Paid amplification begins only after organic or holdout evidence shows qualified response and a working conversion/retention path; preserve separate organic and paid attribution.
- [ ] **T56 — Instrument owned-demand capture.** Every platform path must preserve consent, source, original question, proof consumed, lifecycle stage, next useful action, unsubscribe state, and handoff owner.

### H. Graph, Loop, Harness agent operating system — P1

- [ ] **T57 — Rebuild the Graph around business decisions.** Use fixed nodes for diagnose, evidence, strategy, creative territory, proof production, platform adaptation, review, release, measurement, and learning with explicit branches and rollback.
- [ ] **T58 — Give each node a bounded Loop.** Allow the model to research, draft, critique, compare, and repair inside a node until it meets typed acceptance criteria or returns a truthful blocker.
- [ ] **T59 — Add specialist-agent contracts.** Separate strategist, researcher, cultural analyst, copy chief, creative director, domain expert, media planner, SEO/GEO lead, lifecycle lead, analyst, evaluator, and compliance roles with non-overlapping authority.
- [ ] **T60 — Add adversarial councils at high-leverage decisions.** Require a red team for category strategy, hero concept, central claim, major spend, regulated content, and named-product superiority; disagreement must be resolved with evidence or preserved as a blocker.
- [ ] **T61 — Build a typed artifact graph.** Briefs, evidence, hypotheses, scripts, storyboards, renders, copy, approvals, experiments, and outcomes receive schemas, immutable IDs, versions, provenance, and dependency hashes.
- [ ] **T62 — Strengthen Harness policy enforcement.** Validate required fields, source permissions, action authority, rate limits, privacy, brand rules, platform restrictions, financial caps, audit logs, retries, circuit breakers, and human gates globally.
- [ ] **T63 — Add graceful degradation by capability.** If fresh evidence, a specialist, model, tool, or channel is unavailable, disclose the exact missing capability and narrow the result; never substitute confident generic output.
- [ ] **T64 — Add end-to-end replayability.** Any accepted campaign must reproduce strategy, sources, model/tool versions, prompts, human decisions, asset hashes, release state, and observed outcomes from a sealed receipt.

### I. Experimentation, attribution, and self-evolution — P1

- [ ] **T65 — Create a hypothesis registry.** Every test names mechanism, audience, expected behavior, primary metric, guardrails, minimum sample or time, cost ceiling, stopping rule, and what decision the result will change.
- [ ] **T66 — Separate creative, channel, offer, and audience tests.** Change one major causal family at a time or use a declared factorial design; forbid learning from confounded “everything changed” launches.
- [ ] **T67 — Add leading-to-lagging outcome chains.** Track qualified attention → proof consumption → owned signal → activation → pipeline/purchase → fulfillment → retention/expansion → contribution margin.
- [ ] **T68 — Add causal measurement ladders.** Prefer randomized holdouts, geo or time splits, matched cohorts, incrementality tests, and controlled promotion; use multi-touch attribution only as descriptive evidence when causality is unavailable.
- [ ] **T69 — Build creative wear-out and saturation detection.** Monitor frequency, hook decay, negative response, audience overlap, novelty loss, marginal reach, CAC, and retained value to retire or recombine concepts before brand damage.
- [ ] **T70 — Build approved learning memory.** Store validated mechanism × segment × platform × lifecycle × resource results, rejection reasons, reviewer annotations, and uncertainty; exclude raw generated content and unverified outcomes.
- [ ] **T71 — Add failure-driven data generation.** Convert repeated evaluation failures, buyer objections, lost deals, return reasons, weak cohorts, and platform rejection into new benchmark tasks and training/evaluation examples after review.
- [ ] **T72 — Add strategy portfolio allocation.** Treat mechanisms as a risk-adjusted portfolio: exploit verified performers, explore adjacent hypotheses, reserve capacity for asymmetric experiments, and cap correlated channel risk.

### J. Productization, PK, and release proof — P1/P2

- [ ] **T73 — Build five golden B2B vertical slices.** Manufacturing, construction/engineering, professional services, real estate, and a long-cycle non-software B2B must each run from raw brief to measurable campaign and sales handoff.
- [ ] **T74 — Build five golden B2C vertical slices.** Consumer AI app, health/wellness, beauty/fashion, food/beverage, and travel/local service must each run from insight to native multimodal campaign and retention path.
- [ ] **T75 — Add stage-and-budget stress tests.** Re-run every golden slice at pre-PMF, launch, growth, and mature stages under zero-paid, low, mixed, and scaled budgets; outputs must materially change while preserving strategy truth.
- [ ] **T76 — Expand named-product PK from feature rows to outcome tasks.** Compare strategy diagnosis, hero concept, copy, image/video, platform adaptation, conversion path, measurement plan, and seven-day optimization—not interface checklists.
- [ ] **T77 — Require source-hidden expert and target-buyer review.** Use at least five independent reviewers per comparison, mix craft experts with actual buyer profiles, and report preference, publishability, major rework, trust, recall, and purchase/meeting intent separately.
- [ ] **T78 — Add in-market shadow validation.** Before claiming performance superiority, run authorized low-risk shadow or holdout tests with no automatic spend or publish; record real distribution and retained-business evidence.
- [ ] **T79 — Build an executive control room.** Show strategic diagnosis, current bottleneck, selected mechanisms, rejected alternatives, evidence freshness, asset state, approvals, spend authority, outcome chain, risks, and next decision in one view.
- [ ] **T80 — Define release acceptance.** Ship only when ten golden slices pass functional gates, every claim is traceable, all high-risk actions remain human-gated, 130 named-product rows have valid evidence state, and no “Top 1/4A parity” claim exceeds the available blind and in-market proof.

## Execution order

1. **Gate 0 — Truth and diagnosis:** T01–T16.
2. **Gate 1 — Fresh evidence and strategy:** T17–T32.
3. **Gate 2 — Original creative and multimodal proof:** T33–T48.
4. **Gate 3 — Native distribution and agent runtime:** T49–T64.
5. **Gate 4 — Learning and product proof:** T65–T80.

No later gate can compensate for an earlier failure. More models, GPUs, agents, or assets cannot repair an invalid strategic diagnosis or missing first-party proof.
