# Human-selected omnichannel activation V1

ProofLaunch does not let a model choose the campaign winner. Three locally generated, evidence-bound creative territories first enter six role-hidden comparisons and thirty assignments across five named human reviewers. Only an integrity-complete aggregate may recommend a territory, and a separate authenticated operator must admit that recommendation.

After admission, one deterministic compiler now carries the selected creative system into nine governed lanes:

1. primary channel;
2. secondary channel;
3. landing page;
4. lifecycle email;
5. SEO/GEO answer;
6. community relay;
7. image, video, music and speech production;
8. seven-slot content schedule; and
9. preregistered effect instrumentation.

Every lane retains the selected candidate digest, proof claim, approved proof and human-release boundary. The compiler creates drafts only. It cannot execute a GPU, publish, send, spend, mutate strategy, select a winner or claim an effect.

The activation receipt is content-addressed and deterministically regenerated from the immutable selection and multimodal compilation receipts. A changed candidate, media compilation, lane, schedule, metric or authority flag fails integrity verification.

## Universality regression

The lifecycle matrix runs a clearly labelled synthetic post-selection probe for all 456 supported combinations of industry archetype, B2B/B2C model, lifecycle and budget. Every probe must bind campaign, strategy, challenger plan and proof identity; compile all nine lanes; preserve one creative/evidence spine; and keep every external action disabled. These probes establish contract portability, not creative preference or outcome quality.

The current product has no completed human aggregate, so this compiler is mechanically ready but not yet observed on a real selected winner. Its readiness is not 4A-preference, production-lift or revenue evidence.
