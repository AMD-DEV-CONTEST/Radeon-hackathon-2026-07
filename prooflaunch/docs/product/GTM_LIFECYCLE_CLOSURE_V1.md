# GTM Lifecycle Closure V1

This benchmark verifies that ProofLaunch's strategy, industry adaptation,
channel portfolio, paid-channel constraints, seven-day scheduling, text
challenger, SEO/GEO drafts, video and audio production contracts, analytics,
revenue feedback and Graph/Loop/Harness controls remain connected in one
side-effect-free lifecycle.

The frozen matrix contains 456 industry × business-model × lifecycle × budget
contracts and 1,976 synthetic feedback trajectories:

- qualified positive candidate still requires human review: 456
- insufficient sample: 456
- complaint regression: 456
- negative contribution profit: 456
- zero-budget media-spend breach: 152

Every named failure mode exceeds the 50-case production-readiness floor. Every
zero-budget case mechanically excludes paid channels. Positive mechanics never
activate budget, mutate strategy or publish externally.

This is synthetic regression evidence, not market evidence. It does not prove
publication, reach, annual breakout performance, causal revenue, human
preference, 4A quality, Top-1 standing or Radeon execution.
