# GTM Creative Alpha V1

ProofLaunch now binds strategy and copy generation to a point-in-time creative research contract instead of treating every channel as a formatting target.

## What changes by company

The plan binds industry, B2B/B2C motion, lifecycle, budget, regulation, company scale, the weakest distribution link, content capacity, sales motion, audience and selected channels. Every blind challenger explores three explicitly assigned, mutually distinct style systems; studio contexts may retain a fourth exploration route. Production concurrency remains capacity-bound at two systems for lean teams, three for standard teams and four for studio teams. Regulated contexts exclude the humor route and retain specialist review.

The launch contract also carries an owner-declared product category, product surface, primary audience role and explicit timezone. B2B work models an economic buyer, functional owner, technical evaluator, champion, end user and risk/procurement role, but every asset must choose exactly one primary role instead of writing in committee-average language. B2C work models new users, active users, switchers, advocates and payer/household influence around one concrete use moment. The reference registry covers twelve B2B industry families and eighteen Apple/Google-aligned mobile-app families; it is a bounded language axis, never an automatic or unverifiable store classification. Unknown categories and roles remain `owner_declared_other` until a human maps them.

## Market-language operating system

The market-language compiler is not a persona label attached to generic copy. It binds one category tension, one role decision or use job, one lifecycle job, one budget posture and one platform-native translation into the prompt-visible Creative Alpha plan. It changes the proof object, dominant skepticism, opening, primary formats, qualified signals and bounded next action. Examples include:

- an economic buyer receives decision economics and downside boundaries, while a technical evaluator receives interfaces, failure modes, observability and rollback;
- a B2C switcher receives migration and regret-resolution language, while an advocate receives a voluntarily relayable object that does not depend on referral pressure;
- pre-PMF language is designed to falsify the first-value assumption, launch language proves a narrow wedge, growth protects repeatability and distinctiveness, and mature language seeks defensible renewal and expansion;
- zero-budget work stays within owned, earned, community, partner and product loops; paid reach never counts as organic breakout.

The frozen matrix at `evidence/v10/gtm-market-language-matrix-v1.json` enumerates 5,184 B2B cases and 6,480 B2C cases: 11,664 category × primary-role/state × lifecycle × budget × platform contexts. Every row has a distinct language signature, owner confirmation is required, zero-budget paid activation is zero and automatic external actions are zero. Product surfaces remain runtime-bound across Web app, mobile app, Web Agent, desktop, API, physical product, service and hybrid; the evidence matrix uses Web Agent and mobile app as representative B2B/B2C surfaces rather than inflating coverage by multiplication.

## Craft handoff execution, not a team-shaped prompt

The text challenger now projects the large capability registry into nine content-bound work orders per territory: market mechanism, primary audience decision, message/proof, creative system, two channel-specific producers and three independent reviewers. Producer roles change with the context—for example, a short-video execution can be owned by the short-video director while a LinkedIn execution is owned by the senior social copywriter—and none of the review roles may also be a producer.

The high-quality command path runs three independent advisory reviews per candidate. A `block` fails closed. A `revise` permits exactly one repair while preserving proof, CTA and identity bindings, followed by all three independent reviews again. Even a unanimous advisory pass only makes the candidate eligible for the five-person source-hidden human review; it never selects, releases, publishes, sends or spends.

The frozen matrix at `evidence/v10/gtm-craft-handoff-matrix-v1.json` compiles the real production pack across 144 B2B/B2C × lifecycle × budget × platform contexts and all three territories. It verifies 432 distinct workflow signatures, 3,888 work orders, 18,144 selected atomic-skill assignments, 432/432 producer/reviewer separation passes, 432/432 named-human release requirements and zero automatic external actions. These are structural execution guarantees, not evidence of human preference or market lift.

A real offline Apple Metal run also exercised the strict craft-review boundary with the locally cached `mlx-community/gemma-3-12b-it-4bit` snapshot. The model was loaded once, made 13 review/repair calls, performed zero downloads and zero cloud calls, and used a decision-preserving adapter to bind natural work-order assessments into the closed review contract. Two territories passed all three initial reviews. `decision_xray` received `revise/revise/pass`, produced one mechanically valid repaired candidate, and then received `pass/pass/revise`; the final language reviewer requested a more action-specific relay sentence. Because the one-repair budget was exhausted, the Harness blocked the territory and did not create a human blind-review packet. The adapter only restores immutable identity, approved proof, channels, CTA and disclosure; freezes strategy fields outside the requested repair; removes later exact duplicate blocks; and inserts an allowed platform marker when missing. Every normalization is disclosed in the gateway receipt and the repaired candidate still passes the full semantic Harness before re-review. The sealed receipt is `artifacts/aug3-local-first-gtm-employee-mission-v14/gemma3-12b-text-craft-quality.json`. This proves a real bounded quality loop, not human preference or a craft-quality pass for the portfolio.

## Three-to-six-month growth-case radar

Comparable public or account-authorized aggregates are evaluated inside one category × platform × market cell over 28, 90 and 180 days. A cell needs at least ten cases, and every point-in-time snapshot must be no more than seven days old. The radar uses:

- audience-growth residual against the category benchmark;
- 28-versus-90-day acceleration;
- qualified-engagement and conversion-signal residuals;
- 90/180-day persistence;
- content-supply saturation penalty;
- median/MAD robust scaling clipped to ±3;
- sample-size shrinkage `n/(n+1000)`;
- point-in-time cutoffs and a hard future-leakage gate.

The result is a research-priority hypothesis, never a causal growth claim or a prediction that content will break out. Raw source captions, scripts, transcripts, media and user identifiers are rejected.

## Platform-native copy contract

- Short video: earn the next second, match the first-frame promise, sustain value and measure retention/satisfaction—not empty views.
- LinkedIn: give a decision-maker one original, defensible insight that can enter a real operating conversation.
- Search: answer the decision job directly, add first-hand value and evidence, and avoid mass-produced search-first copy.
- Communities: listen first, respect local norms, contribute a useful object and design for participation rather than extraction.

The owner-declared distribution platform adds a second, exact overlay. TikTok is spoken, immediate and participation-ready; Instagram is visually led and save/share useful; YouTube is promise-matched and retention-sustaining; LinkedIn is operator-specific and defensible; Reddit is community-contextual; X is compressed, timely and point-of-view led. Each has its own opening, preferred content forms, quality signals and anti-patterns. Unknown platforms fall back to a generic native-format contract rather than pretending an unsupported algorithm rule.

## Distinctive style portfolio

The context compiler selects three to four exploration systems from documentary proof, expert teardown, operator memo, restrained humor, category inversion, participatory ritual, founder candor, useful tool, visual metaphor and case reconstruction. Every candidate keeps one approved proof spine but must use a distinct structure, voice and participation mechanism. Cross-platform reformatting and source imitation are rejected.

The transfer boundary allows audience tension, proof sequence, format mechanic, participation mechanic, distribution bridge and measurement hypothesis. It forbids source wording, signature characters, shot-for-shot structure, visual trade dress and creator voice imitation. A candidate must recombine at least three independent dimensions and remains subject to semantic/expression-overlap review.

## Bounded self-evolution

The learner receives qualified attention, brand distinctiveness, downstream action, negative-feedback and verified conversion/revenue observations over 7/28/90/180-day horizons. It uses time decay, change-point reset, drift detection, holdouts and off-policy replay. Failures are indexed by market domain, role capability, atomic skill and one of eight difficulty types; they do not become training data automatically. A named human must adjudicate the failure before a gold-repair prefix may enter offline shadow replay. The learner may produce only a next-run policy candidate. It cannot mutate the current run, publish, allocate spend or promote itself without named human review.

## Truth boundary

The implementation proves deterministic context binding, 11,664-way language differentiation mechanics, a 22-role/220-skill craft decomposition, 144 deterministic staffing contexts, comparable-case ranking mechanics, prompt binding, integrity validation and human release gates. It does not prove which public brand is growing fastest, human-level craft, creative preference, production lift, revenue causality or superiority over a named product. Those claims require admitted live aggregates, walk-forward results and source-hidden human evaluation.
