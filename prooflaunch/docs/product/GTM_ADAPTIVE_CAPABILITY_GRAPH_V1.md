## What changes per company

Business model, lifecycle, budget, regulation, scale, distribution strength, content capacity and sales motion alter lane priority, growth routes, candidate breadth and repair depth. The approval, rights, trace and external-action boundaries do not change.

## Truth boundary

This matrix proves deterministic differentiation and bounded execution contracts over 6,912 synthetic context combinations. It does not prove universal market fit, authorized data access, installation or superiority of shadow candidates, creative preference, named-product wins, virality, 4A quality, production lift or causal revenue. Every external action and strategy change remains human-gated.
