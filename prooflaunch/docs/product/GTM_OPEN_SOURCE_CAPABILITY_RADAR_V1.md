# GTM Open-Source Capability Radar V2

Observed: 2026-08-01  
Deadline path: 2026-08-03  
Machine-readable source: `config/gtm_open_source_capability_radar_v1.json`

## Outcome

The product now has one governed selection map spanning 60 product capability lanes, 111 pinned open-source repositories, 20 replaceable model/evaluator routes, the algorithms required by each lane, and a lane-specific effect gate. This is a selection and admission contract, not a claim that 111 projects have been installed or that their marketing claims have been reproduced.

The second pass adds the missing last-mile employee systems: deterministic browser and sandboxed computer use; CRM/ABM and customer-success loops; marketing connectors; localization, transcreation and accessibility; landing-page CRO; brand and claim governance; competitive intelligence; creator/PR decisions; editable documents and slides; durable multi-agent execution; executable Agent environments; contribution-margin feedback; multimodal memory; and a compact unified-multimodal challenger. Each remains behind the existing Graph–Loop–Harness rather than becoming a new control plane.

## Second-pass source conclusions

- Microsoft Playwright is the deterministic browser baseline. Browser-use, UI-TARS Desktop, ShowUI, MobileAgent and Open Cowork remain sandboxed challengers; no GUI model may bypass authentication, captcha, paywalls or platform controls.
- Google AX is promising but explicitly early-stage. Dapr Agents, Microsoft Agent Framework and DeerFlow contribute replay, actor, handoff, sandbox and long-horizon patterns; none has earned a deadline migration.
- MemOS and Neo4j Agent Memory add multimodal, graph and reasoning-memory hypotheses. They must beat the incumbent on temporal recall, citation, contradiction, tenant isolation and exact deletion.
- PresentBench adds a reproducible advertising-collateral slice, but its model judges cannot replace the frozen expert blind review.
- NVIDIA NeMo Evaluator and BrowserGym strengthen reproducible model/Agent evaluation and executable environments. Dataset and backend terms remain separate admission gates.
- Lance is a compact unified image/video understanding-generation-editing research checkpoint. The upstream quality and resolution caveats make it a challenger, not a replacement for the specialist Qwen-Image, Wan and Qwen3-TTS paths.
- Twenty, Mautic, Activepieces and Weblate have mixed or reciprocal licensing implications; they enter as isolated product/interface references until component-level legal review passes. Chatwoot, axe-core and Playwright have clearer narrow adapter paths.
- Revenue learning now requires reconciled net revenue, refunds, chargebacks, COGS, fulfillment, platform, media, model and operator costs. Qualified pipeline never counts as revenue, and a model cannot activate budget.

The architecture remains:

```text
deterministic Graph
  -> bounded Loop inside selected nodes
  -> global Harness around every model, tool, memory write, publish and spend action
```

The Aug 3 path does not migrate the proven ProofLaunch runtime to another orchestration framework. Pydantic contracts, OpenTelemetry traces, public-source adapters, deterministic extractors and mechanical evaluation can enter through thin boundaries. Temporal, LangGraph, Graphiti, Mem0, DSPy optimizers and online RL enter only through shadow or post-deadline evaluation until they beat the incumbent on frozen evidence.

## Full-scenario extension

The original 30 lanes covered the core GTM lifecycle. A second official-repository pass exposed 15 cross-cutting gaps that determine whether the system can operate as an employee rather than a demo:

- point-in-time data contracts, feature consistency and OpenLineage-compatible lineage;
- layout-aware enterprise document parsing, graph/vector retrieval and citation-span verification;
- CLV, churn, retention, personalization, next-best-action, pricing and choice modeling;
- community diffusion, time-series forecasting, anomaly and capacity planning;
- unified multimodal evaluation with paired confidence intervals and calibrated human adjudication;
- policy-as-code, PII protection, C2PA content provenance and rights-bound asset history;
- reproducible ComfyUI production graphs plus structured human annotation;
- embedded analytics, consented identity resolution, measured SEO/GEO operations;
- bounded multi-objective optimization, drift monitoring and champion-challenger rollback.

These are now lanes 31–45 in the machine-readable radar. Lanes 46–60 close employee execution, revenue and multimodal-product gaps. Each names algorithms, pinned repositories, a deadline decision and an effect gate. Most are deliberately `evaluate`, `shadow` or `isolate`: repository discovery does not authorize dependency installation, dataset use, publishing, spend, identity enrichment, price changes or self-modification.

## Critical model and project corrections

1. `stepfun-ai/Step-Audio` is not the speech default because upstream marks it unmaintained. `QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS` becomes the primary speech candidate. `Step-Audio-EditX` is an expressive editing candidate, but upstream documents NVIDIA/CUDA testing; it is not Radeon evidence.
2. Music points to the active `ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5` repository rather than the older ACE-Step route.
3. Qwen-Image 2.0 is the image target. The captured ComfyUI workflow is still Qwen-Image-2512, so those are two different truth states and must not be conflated.
4. Wan2.2 is the permissive video fallback candidate. LTX-2.3 remains an optional quality route, but its community license has commercial revenue and competitive-use conditions; it cannot be a default commercial dependency without legal clearance.
5. MiniMax H3 remains unverified because no authoritative repository or observed AMD model identifier was sealed. The runtime must use the official AMD `/models` result instead of inventing an identifier.
6. FLUX.2 and MOVA are challengers, not replacements for the already captured Qwen-Image/Wan/ACE routes. They need separate weight terms, Radeon receipts and blind preference evidence.
7. `lmms-eval` can unify mechanical multimodal evaluation, but its task datasets and model backends require separate version and license review. It cannot replace the frozen campaign-specific human rubric.
8. OpenOneRec and RecBole are research references. Offline ranking gains do not authorize personalized activation; cold-start, diversity, privacy, fairness and randomized incremental lift remain gates.
9. OPA, Presidio and C2PA enter as sidecars behind the existing Harness. They do not bypass the current content-bound approval, tenant isolation or evidence packet.

## Non-negotiable admission boundary

An official repository and a source-code license do not grant model, dataset, training-corpus, voice, likeness, font or output rights. Every dependency also needs a pinned version, isolated interface, failure policy, rollback path and measured advantage. No generated media is “4A,” no product is “Top-1,” and no campaign caused revenue until the corresponding real, external evidence exists.

The local FFmpeg/basic renderer remains an offline fixture and transport utility. It is not the creative quality path and receives no further deadline investment unless a production workflow is blocked on muxing or validation.
