## Outcome

The rejected Klaviyo-colored card catalogue was removed from the home route. The new home page is one four-act GTM mission with a distinct composition for each act:

1. **Mission opening** — an asymmetric statement, evidence-bound brief and real Frontier Studio surface.
2. **The work** — two continuous product scenes using the real production studio and lifecycle cockpit.
3. **The native cut** — a B2B/B2C campaign contact sheet whose channel cuts change position, rhythm and argument instead of repeating equal cards.
4. **Operating depth and human line** — an orbital Front/Middle/Back control model followed by a full-width release boundary.

The bottom no longer repeats the prior three-card product surface and four-card priority queue. The operating-depth section is a spatial system; the release section is a single dark editorial field with a sequenced human decision line.
