# 4K image mastering execution contract V1

This workstream reuses the control ideas of FLUX.2 multi-reference editing and
prompt upsampling, Grounding DINO product grounding, SAM 2.1 masks and mature
restoration pipelines without claiming that a repository name or planned route
is an executed model result.

The verified fixture is a real 3840×2160 project-owned Pillow composition. It
creates source-bound product, logo and text masks; keeps product pixels exact;
renders logo and typography directly at the final canvas resolution; produces
a no-text Google version and a social-safe version; and blocks repair outside a
small observed failure region or over a protected layer. It also freezes brand
color, font hash, composition, lighting, material and negative-space contracts,
a brand-memory LoRA deny rule, and a hard-gated five-reward Best-of-N selector.

This mechanical fixture is not a FLUX.2, SDXL, Grounding DINO, SAM2.1,
Real-ESRGAN, SwinIR or SeedVR2 run. Accordingly, TODO 0901–0904, 0906–0910,
0920–0921, 0923–0924, 0931–0935, 0941–0945 and 0947–0950 remain open until
their model artifacts, outputs, comparisons and independent quality evidence
exist. Model names and research results are not reproduced gains.
