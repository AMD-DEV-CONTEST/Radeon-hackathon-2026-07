# Frontier execution protocol for all 1,500 tasks

**Frozen protocol:** `frontier-20-lens-v3`  
**Scope:** V9 tasks 0001–1200 and V10 tasks 1201–1500  
**Purpose:** turn “world-top-team quality” into falsifiable engineering
requirements instead of a prestige claim.

## Public-method lenses

The canonical twenty-lens synthesis is
[`V10_20_LENS_SYNTHESIS_2026-07-29.md`](V10_20_LENS_SYNTHESIS_2026-07-29.md).
The names are public research and product-method coordinates, not simulated
personas, endorsements, or rankings.

Every applicable task must combine the smallest relevant set of these methods:

- latent/world-state modelling, bounded search, spatial intelligence, sparse
  hardware-aware specialization, native multimodal curricula, rich-feedback
  adaptation, offline/interactive learning, and reproducible multi-axis eval;
- durable state, bounded specialists, direct manipulation, an end-to-end
  content supply chain, sandboxed tools, explicit approval, rollback, and
  observed GTM outcomes.

Adding agents, prompts, adapters, training stages, or screens is not a
breakthrough by itself.

## Mandatory record per task

Each task record must preserve the canonical `todo_id` and include:

1. **Reuse ledger:** real upstream source, pinned revision, verified license,
   maintenance state, ROCm feasibility, integration seam, and what is not
   being rewritten.
2. **Frozen baseline:** the strongest executable alternative on the same
   inputs, tools, hardware, and evidence boundary.
3. **Mechanism delta:** the smallest non-obvious change and why it is not
   generic multi-agent orchestration, prompt wrapping, routine SFT/LoRA, more
   tokens, or UI veneer.
4. **Falsifier:** ablation, negative control, adversarial case, metric, budget,
   stop/elimination rule, and rollback.
5. **4A behavior:** the concrete agency task that changes for a strategist,
   creative, producer, account lead, analyst, or approver and can be judged
   blind.
6. **Radeon boundary:** what executes on the multi-GPU Radeon/ROCm path, the
   per-rank VRAM and wall-clock budget, the frozen single-GPU equivalence
   baseline, and what remains external teacher material. Direct multi-GPU
   evidence requires at least two physical GPUs and RCCL receipts.
7. **Truth class:** local mechanism, trained-model observation, independent
   human result, Radeon trace, or observed business outcome. One class cannot
   substitute for another.

Unknown revisions, licenses, rules, metrics, or source URLs must be marked
`HOLD`. Placeholders and inferred licenses are invalid evidence.

## Innovation gate

Breakthrough candidates are proposed on four 0–5 axes:

- technical mechanism delta;
- user-visible GTM craft/product delta;
- falsifiability and evidence quality;
- multi-GPU Radeon feasibility, correctness equivalence, and measured
  parallel efficiency.

A candidate below 16/20 or below 3 on any axis remains `HOLD/REJECT`.
Self-scoring never proves the gate passed; the score is admitted only after the
named implementation, ablation, and required blind or external evidence exist.
Compliance, provenance, safety, and administrative tasks may be classified as
non-innovation foundations. They must be correct and complete, but irrelevant
complexity cannot be added to manufacture an innovation score.

## Nine-stage official-teacher pipeline

AMD Radeon Token Factory drives a source-bound development corpus:

`Architect + Research → Adversarial Critic → Implementer → Verifier + 4A Critic → Synthesis → Frontier Judge → Final Repair`

- Architect and research start independently.
- Critic requires both.
- Implementer requires the critic.
- Verifier requires the implementation record.
- 4A critic requires implementation plus verification.
- Synthesis requires implementation, verification, and 4A critique.
- An independent Frontier Judge red-teams the synthesis for ordinary
  orchestration/training theatre, source fabrication, weak falsifiers, circular
  scores, evidence substitution, and implausible Radeon budgets.
- Final Repair must preserve every blocker and judge correction in strict JSON;
  it still cannot mark a task complete.

Every visible output is content-addressed. Truncation, empty visible output,
official-source placeholders, inferred licenses, score-gate gaming, missing
prerequisites, or credential patterns block the episode. Hidden reasoning is
not persisted. The validator is
[`scripts/validate_tokenfactory_corpus.py`](../scripts/validate_tokenfactory_corpus.py).

The online corpus is private `external_teacher_advisory`. It can improve
plans, critique, and evaluation design. It can become training data only after
the provider-output rights gate is explicitly unblocked; the current audit is
`rights_review_required`. It cannot close a TODO, prove a local Radeon run,
establish 4A preference, or support a business-lift claim.

## Top-10 4A campaign-transfer overlay

The generic 4A critic is followed by four specialist passes over every
two-TODO batch:

`Deep Strategy → Deep Craft → Effectiveness Red-Team → Transfer Synthesis`

The reference cohort is date-pinned to ten North-American agencies and stores
13 complete, individually source-located 16-layer campaign abstractions. Every
agency has at least one complete case and at least five observed channels in
the seed set. Observed statements remain distinct from inference; reported
case outcomes remain distinct from causal evidence.

The final transfer record must contain the exact TODO ID, frozen case IDs,
observed evidence, strategic transfer, creative mechanism, channel/craft
contract, effectiveness test, negative control, non-copy boundary, research
partition, and admission state. Unknown case IDs, identity-copy instructions,
missing negative controls, or a changed partition block the batch. All
synthesis records share one content-addressed Top-10 context. The validator is
[`scripts/validate_four_a_transfer_corpus.py`](../scripts/validate_four_a_transfer_corpus.py).

The 13-case seed is not relabeled as the 300-case acceptance corpus. The
30-distinct-cases-per-agency acquisition gate stays open until all 300 have
individual locators, 16 layers, deduplication, channel coverage, and sealed
evaluation clearance.

## Anti-theatre acceptance

The following are automatic rejection signals:

- an invented repository, paper, revision, license, score, human rating, or
  Radeon trace;
- a “swarm” without one authoritative state owner, bounded parallel value,
  permissions, budget, stop rules, and replay-safe side effects;
- “model training” without rights-audited data, split integrity, frozen
  baselines, ablations, retention tests, rollback, and a Radeon receipt;
- “4K” established by dimensions alone rather than OCR, color, identity,
  temporal, audio/visual, rights, approval, and claim-locator gates;
- a simulated or predicted outcome presented as observed or causal;
- an internal self-review presented as independent North American 4A evidence.

A negative result with a sealed falsifier and rollback may close a research
task. It must not be promoted into the product path.
