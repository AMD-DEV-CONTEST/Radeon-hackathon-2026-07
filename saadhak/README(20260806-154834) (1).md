# FrameGuard

**Multimodal video privacy for sensitive content and human faces**

**Hackathon track:** Multimodal AI

FrameGuard analyzes video and audio, locates privacy-sensitive content, and produces a redacted MP4 with an auditable record of every action. The product is organized around two independent workflows:

1. **Secret Protection** - detect and redact credentials, personal data, internal identifiers, QR codes, and spoken sensitive information.
2. **Face Privacy** - detect people, group repeated face tracks into identities, and blur selected faces across the video.

Both workflows share the same media ingestion, localization, rendering, reporting, and privacy-safe logging layers.

![FrameGuard architecture](docs/architecture.png)

## Product capabilities

### 1. Secret Protection

FrameGuard inspects both visible frames and spoken audio. It combines semantic multimodal reasoning with deterministic text detection so that contextual and structured secrets can be handled in the same pipeline.

Detected content can include:

- API keys, access tokens, and credentials
- Email addresses and phone numbers
- IPv4 and IPv6 addresses
- Customer, account, and internal system identifiers
- Private URLs and QR codes
- Sensitive information spoken in the audio track

The output preserves the surrounding video while blurring the localized visual secret and muting the relevant audio interval.

![Secret protection before and after](docs/screenshots/secret-protection-before-after.png)

#### Secret-protection workflow

```text
Uploaded MP4
    |
    +-- split into short video chunks
    +-- extract mono WAV audio with FFmpeg
    |
    v
Qwen2.5-Omni semantic analysis
    +-- visible secrets
    +-- spoken secrets
    +-- contextual identifiers
    |
    v
OCR and deterministic pattern checks
    +-- exact text localization
    +-- structured-value validation
    +-- QR-code localization
    |
    v
Normalize, merge, and deduplicate findings
    |
    +-- visual finding -> blur, pixelate, or blackout
    +-- audio finding  -> mute the time interval
    |
    v
Redacted MP4 + JSON audit report + JSONL run log
```

### 2. Face Privacy

FrameGuard detects faces throughout a video and associates detections across time. Repeated or fragmented tracks are grouped into user-reviewable identity profiles. The user can then control face redaction without depending on automatic age or identity assumptions.

Supported actions include:

- Blur every detected face
- Blur selected gallery profiles
- Keep selected profiles visible and blur everyone else
- Upload one or more reference photos and blur matching profiles
- Upload reference photos that should remain visible

The profile gallery shows a clear selected state and reports how many raw face-track fragments were merged into each apparent identity.

![Face gallery selection](docs/screenshots/face-gallery-selection.png)

Selected identities are mapped back to their temporal face tracks. FrameGuard interpolates observations between sampled frames and applies blur consistently across the video.

![Selective face blur](docs/screenshots/face-blur-before-after.png)

#### Face-privacy workflow

```text
Uploaded MP4
    |
    v
YuNet face detection
    +-- face bounding box
    +-- detector confidence
    +-- five facial landmarks
    |
    v
Temporal face tracking
    +-- associate nearby detections
    +-- preserve track IDs
    +-- reject short unstable tracks
    |
    v
SFace identity representation
    +-- align face crops
    +-- compute normalized embeddings
    +-- merge compatible non-overlapping fragments
    |
    v
User-reviewed face gallery
    +-- select people to blur
    +-- select people to keep visible
    +-- match uploaded reference photos
    |
    v
Track-aware blur rendering
    |
    v
Redacted MP4 + face-selection audit metadata
```

## Technical architecture

### Media ingestion

FFmpeg and OpenCV provide deterministic media handling:

- Video metadata extraction
- Chunk generation for multimodal analysis
- Audio extraction to WAV
- Frame sampling for OCR, QR detection, and face detection
- Final MP4 assembly and audio muting

### Multimodal secret detection

FrameGuard sends video and audio as separate multimodal inputs to **Qwen2.5-Omni-3B** through an OpenAI-compatible **vLLM** endpoint. The model returns structured findings containing the content type, modality, time interval, confidence, and reason.

The response parser:

- Accepts JSON objects and arrays
- Removes markdown fences
- Validates finding fields
- Converts local chunk timestamps into video timestamps
- Clamps invalid intervals
- Expands zero-length intervals
- Rejects malformed findings

### Deterministic text and QR detection

Semantic detection is reinforced by deterministic scanning:

- OCR extracts text and word-level bounding boxes
- Regular expressions validate structured secrets
- QR detection returns exact visual coordinates
- Findings from multiple detectors are merged and deduplicated

This hybrid design separates two responsibilities:

- Qwen identifies **what is sensitive and why**
- OCR and visual detectors identify **where it appears**

### Face detection and tracking

**YuNet** runs through OpenCV's `FaceDetectorYN` interface. Each sampled frame produces face boxes, landmarks, and confidence scores. FrameGuard associates detections across time using spatial overlap, motion continuity, and maximum-gap rules.

The tracker outputs stable face-track findings containing timestamped box observations. These observations are later interpolated by the renderer to reduce flicker between sampled frames.

### Face grouping and reference matching

**SFace** runs through OpenCV's `FaceRecognizerSF` interface.

For each face-track fragment, FrameGuard:

1. Selects several candidate observations.
2. Scores the candidates using original face size, image sharpness, and YuNet confidence.
3. Aligns the strongest face crops.
4. Computes normalized SFace embeddings.
5. Averages multiple strong embeddings for the fragment.
6. Groups compatible fragments that are visually similar and do not coexist in time.

Uploaded reference photos are processed through the same YuNet and SFace path. Matching is based on cosine similarity against the gallery profiles. The result is presented for user review rather than treated as identity verification.

### Redaction renderer

All detectors produce a common finding schema containing:

- Finding type
- Modality
- Start and end times
- Confidence
- Redaction action
- Detection sources
- Timestamped visual observations

The renderer applies:

- Gaussian blur, pixelation, or blackout to visual regions
- Temporal interpolation for tracked face boxes
- FFmpeg audio muting for sensitive spoken intervals
- Final video encoding with the original non-sensitive content preserved

### Audit and observability

Every run creates:

- A redacted MP4
- A JSON audit report
- A privacy-safe JSONL execution log

![Audit report](docs/screenshots/audit-report.png)

The audit report records detection types, timing, localization, confidence, action, source, profile selection, and match metadata. Raw face embeddings and gallery crops are not written to the report.

## Privacy model

FrameGuard is designed to avoid creating additional privacy exposure while performing redaction.

- Exact secret values are hidden from normal logs.
- Raw Qwen responses are excluded by default.
- Face crops remain in the active application session.
- SFace embeddings remain in memory and are not persisted to the audit report.
- Uploaded reference photos are used for the current matching operation and are not embedded in output metadata.
- Reports use anonymous profile IDs and track IDs rather than names.

## Evaluation criteria

### Secret Protection

- Secret recall by type
- Visual localization accuracy
- Audio interval accuracy
- False-redaction rate
- Coverage from semantic and deterministic detectors
- Rendered-output verification

### Face Privacy

- Face-detection recall
- Track stability across motion and occlusion
- Duplicate-profile rate
- Incorrect identity-merge rate
- Reference-photo match accuracy
- Blur continuity and flicker rate
- User selection correctness

## Known limitations

- Very small, blurred, profile, or occluded faces may not produce clear gallery images.
- Face tracking can fragment during fast motion or long occlusion.
- SFace similarity supports user-assisted grouping and matching; it is not identity verification.
- OCR performance depends on resolution, contrast, font size, and motion blur.
- Semantic detection can miss unusual secret formats; deterministic patterns provide only partial coverage.
- Redaction quality must be evaluated on the rendered output, not only on detector reports.
