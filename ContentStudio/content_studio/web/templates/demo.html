🚀 Core Features

📝

AI Writing

Qwen / GLM LLMs (AMD GPU) generate full-length WeChat articles and Xiaohongshu posts with titles, summaries, body text, and image captions.

🎨

Photo Style Transfer

Upload photos and transform them into anime, cyberpunk, watercolor, oil painting, ink wash, pixel art styles via ComfyUI (AMD GPU). Per-photo style selection.

👁️

Vision-Based Placement

The vision model reads each photo's content (text on screenshots, scenes) and automatically places it in the most relevant section of the article.

🖼️

AI Text-to-Image

ComfyUI generates brand-new illustrations based on the article theme, supplementing photos with original artwork.

📱

Direct Draft Publishing

One click sends the complete article with images to WeChat Official Account draft box via official API. No copy-paste needed.

✏️

Editable Preview

Drag images to any position, edit body text, regenerate if not satisfied. Full control before publishing.
