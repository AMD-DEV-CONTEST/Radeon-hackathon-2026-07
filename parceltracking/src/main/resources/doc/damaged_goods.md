## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the procedures for handling shipments that arrive damaged while in the custody of Apex Tracking. Our goal is to ensure fair, consistent, and timely resolution for all customers affected by damaged goods while protecting the company against fraudulent claims.

## 2. Definitions

- **Visible Damage:** External damage to the package that is apparent upon delivery, such as crushed corners, punctures, tears, water stains, dents, or crushed boxes.
- **Concealed Damage:** Damage to the contents that is not visible from the outside of the package and is discovered only after opening. This includes broken items, crushed components, liquid spills, or malfunctioning electronics.
- **Declared Value:** The monetary value of the shipment contents as declared by the sender at the time of booking. This value determines the maximum liability of Apex Tracking.
- **Third-Party Damage:** Damage caused by another carrier after Apex Tracking has transferred the package to a partner carrier for final delivery.
- **Total Loss:** A shipment where the contents are completely destroyed, unsalvageable, or where the cost of repair exceeds the declared value.

## 3. Reporting Requirements

### 3.1 Visible Damage
Recipients must note visible damage on the delivery receipt or electronic signature capture device before signing or accepting the package. Photographs of the damaged package from multiple angles must be taken at the time of delivery. Claims for visible damage must be filed within 48 hours of delivery.

**What to document:**
- Photograph the exterior of the box showing the damage
- Photograph the interior packing materials
- Photograph the damaged item(s) in their original position
- Note the condition on the driver's delivery tablet

### 3.2 Concealed Damage
For concealed damage, recipients must retain all packaging materials, including the outer box, inner cushioning, and any inserts, in their original condition. Claims must be filed within 5 business days of delivery. The original box and packing materials must be available for inspection by an Apex Tracking representative. Failure to preserve the packaging may result in automatic claim denial.

### 3.3 High-Value Shipments
For shipments with a declared value exceeding $1,000, Apex Tracking requires a signature upon delivery. If the package was left without a signature and damage is discovered, the claim process may be more complex and additional verification will be required.

## 4. Compensation Structure

| Damage Type | Compensation | Maximum Payout | Processing Time |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Partial Damage | Repair cost or depreciated value | $2,000 | 7 business days |
| Total Loss | Full declared value | $2,000 (or insured amount) | 10 business days |
| Visible Damage | Full declared value | $2,000 | 5 business days |
| Concealed Damage | Full declared value | $2,000 | 10 business days |

Compensation is calculated based on the declared value of the shipment, not to exceed $2,000 per package unless additional insurance was purchased at the time of booking. For shipments with purchased insurance, the full insured value will be honored up to $25,000 per package. Insurance premiums are non-refundable.

## 5. Inspection Process

Apex Tracking reserves the right to inspect damaged packages and their packaging before any claim is paid. Our inspection team will assess whether the damage occurred during transit or was due to inadequate packing by the sender.

**Inspection criteria include:**
- Adequacy of cushioning material (minimum 2 inches on all sides for fragile items)
- Box integrity and whether the correct box strength was used
- Presence of proper labeling such as "Fragile" or "This Side Up"
- Evidence of prior damage or repackaging
- Signs of water exposure, crushing, or mishandling

If the inspection determines that inadequate packing caused the damage, the claim will be denied and the sender will be notified with recommendations for proper packing procedures.

## 6. Exclusions

Claims will be denied for damage caused by the following:

- Improper, insufficient, or inadequate packing by the sender
- Damage to items specifically excluded from coverage including liquids, glassware, artwork, antiques, or electronics without proper original packaging or custom cushioning
- Damage resulting from prohibited items as listed in our Terms of Service, including hazardous materials, flammable substances, and illegal goods
- Pre-existing damage that was present before the package was handed to Apex Tracking
- Damage caused by the recipient during the unpacking process
- Normal wear and tear or cosmetic imperfections that do not affect functionality
- Items that are inherently fragile and not packaged according to our published guidelines

## 7. Filing a Claim

### Online Submission
Claims can be submitted online at www.apextracking.com/claims. Our online portal allows you to upload all required documentation, track the status of your claim in real time, and communicate directly with the claims adjuster assigned to your case.

### Email Submission
Claims can also be submitted by emailing claims@apextracking.com with the subject line "Damaged Goods Claim - [Tracking Number]".

### Required Documentation
- The tracking number for the shipment
- Clear photographs of the damage from multiple angles
- Photographs of the packaging showing the condition upon arrival
- The original receipt, invoice, or proof of purchase showing the item value
- A detailed description of the damage and how it was discovered
- The delivery receipt or proof of delivery document

## 8. Dispute Resolution

If a customer disagrees with the outcome of a claim, they may request a formal review by submitting a written appeal to appeals@apextracking.com within 30 days of the claim decision. The appeal will be reviewed by a senior claims adjuster who was not involved in the original decision. A final determination will be provided within 15 business days.

## 9. Fraud Prevention

Apex Tracking employs advanced analytics and pattern recognition to identify potentially fraudulent claims. Customers found to have submitted fraudulent claims will have their accounts permanently suspended and may be referred to law enforcement authorities. Fraud indicators include repeated claims from the same address, claims for high-value items without proper insurance, and claims with inconsistent documentation.
