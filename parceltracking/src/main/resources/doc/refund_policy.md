## 1. Overview

This Refund Policy governs all refund requests for shipping services provided by Apex Tracking ("Company"). Customers may be eligible for a full or partial refund under the conditions outlined below. This policy applies to all domestic and international shipments processed through our platform.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

### 2.1 Late Delivery Refunds
If a shipment is delivered more than 48 hours after the guaranteed delivery date, the customer is eligible for a full refund of shipping charges. Claims must be submitted within 14 calendar days of the actual delivery date. The guaranteed delivery date is defined as the date provided at the time of booking and confirmed via email confirmation.

**Example:** A package with a guaranteed delivery date of August 10 arrives on August 13. The customer is entitled to a full refund of shipping costs.

### 2.2 Lost Package Refunds
Packages that have not been delivered or updated in the tracking system for 10 consecutive business days are considered lost. Customers may file a lost package claim after this period. Refunds for lost packages include the full declared value of the contents plus all shipping charges, up to a maximum of $5,000 per shipment. For high-value items, customers are strongly encouraged to purchase additional insurance at the time of booking.

**Required Documentation for Lost Claims:**
- Proof of value (receipt, invoice, or appraisal)
- Screenshot of the final tracking status
- Sender and recipient contact information
- Description of the contents

### 2.3 Incorrect Delivery Refunds
If a package is delivered to the wrong address due to an error by Apex Tracking, the customer is entitled to a full refund of shipping charges plus a 20% credit toward the next shipment. The customer must provide photographic evidence of the incorrect delivery location.

### 2.4 Duplicate Payment Refunds
If a customer is charged more than once for the same shipment due to a system error, the duplicate charges will be refunded in full within 3 business days of notification.

### 2.5 Cancellation Refunds
Shipments cancelled before pickup are eligible for a full refund. Shipments cancelled after pickup but before the first scan are eligible for a 50% refund. No refunds are issued once the package has been scanned into the sorting facility.

## 3. Exclusions

Refunds will not be issued for the following circumstances:

- Delays caused by natural disasters, extreme weather, hurricanes, earthquakes, floods, wildfires, or acts of God
- Shipments with incorrect or incomplete addresses provided by the sender
- Packages seized, detained, or opened by customs, border protection, or government authorities
- Perishable goods that spoil due to delays beyond our operational control
- Shipments containing prohibited or hazardous materials as defined in our Terms of Service
- Delays caused by the recipient being unavailable for delivery after multiple attempts
- Packages refused by the recipient without valid reason
- Shipments where the declared value was misrepresented or falsified

## 4. Claim Process

### Step 1: Initiate the Claim
Customers must contact our support team at support@apextracking.com or call 1-800-555-0199. Our support hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST, and Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.

### Step 2: Provide Documentation
Claims must include the tracking number, a detailed description of the issue, and supporting documentation such as photographs of the package, delivery confirmation screenshots, or proof of value.

### Step 3: Claim Review
Our claims team will review the submission within 2 business days. We may request additional information or documentation during this review period.

### Step 4: Resolution
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of claim approval and will be credited to the original payment method. Customers will receive a confirmation email once the refund has been issued.
