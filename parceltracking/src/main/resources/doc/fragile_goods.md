## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the specific procedures, packaging requirements, refund processes, insurance claim steps, and liability waivers for shipments containing fragile items handled by Apex Tracking. Fragile goods require special handling, packaging, and documentation to ensure safe transit and fair claims resolution.

## 2. Definitions

- **Fragile Item:** Any item that is susceptible to breakage, cracking, scratching, or damage from impact, vibration, compression, or temperature changes during transit. This includes but is not limited to glassware, ceramics, electronics, artwork, musical instruments, antiques, marble, stone, and laboratory equipment.
- **Specialized Packaging:** Packaging that meets or exceeds Apex Tracking's published standards for fragile item shipment, including double-walled boxes, custom foam inserts, air cushioning, and appropriate outer labeling.
- **Declared Fragile Value:** The declared monetary value of the fragile item(s) at the time of booking, which must be itemized per piece for multi-item shipments.
- **Liability Waiver:** A legal document signed by the sender acknowledging that Apex Tracking's liability is limited when the sender declines specialized packaging or additional insurance for fragile items.
- **Pre-Transit Inspection:** A mandatory inspection of the packaging and item condition conducted by an Apex Tracking agent before the shipment is accepted for transit.

## 3. Fragile Item Classification

### 3.1 Category A: Standard Fragile
Items that require basic protective packaging but have a lower risk of damage:
- Ceramic mugs and dinnerware (properly wrapped)
- Picture frames (with protective corner guards)
- Small electronics (in original packaging)
- Books and documents

### 3.2 Category B: High-Risk Fragile
Items with a high probability of damage if not packaged to exact specifications:
- Glassware, crystal, and mirrors
- LCD/LED screens and monitors
- Musical instruments (guitars, violins, keyboards)
- Marble, stone, and granite items
- Laboratory glassware and equipment

### 3.3 Category C: Extreme-Risk Fragile
Items that require specialized crating, temperature control, or additional handling:
- Antiques and heirlooms over 100 years old
- Fine art, paintings, and sculptures
- Chandeliers and large glass fixtures
- Medical imaging equipment and sensitive electronics
- Custom or one-of-a-kind items

## 4. Mandatory Packaging Requirements

### 4.1 General Packaging Standards
All fragile items must adhere to the following minimum packaging standards:

| Requirement | Specification |
|-------------|---------------|
| Outer Box | Double-walled corrugated cardboard, minimum 275 lb test strength |
| Cushioning | Minimum 3 inches of cushioning material on all sides (foam, bubble wrap, or air pillows) |
| Inner Wrapping | Each individual item must be wrapped separately in bubble wrap (minimum 2 layers) |
| Box Fill | No empty space — all voids must be filled with cushioning material |
| Sealing | Box must be sealed with pressure-sensitive tape (minimum 3 strips on top and bottom) |
| Labeling | "FRAGILE" labels on all six sides of the box; "THIS SIDE UP" arrows on two adjacent sides |

### 4.2 Category-Specific Requirements

**Category A:** Standard packaging as outlined above. Original manufacturer packaging is acceptable if it includes all original foam inserts and is placed inside an outer shipping box.

**Category B:** In addition to standard packaging, items must be double-boxed (inner box with 2 inches of cushioning placed inside an outer box with 2 inches of additional cushioning). Each item must be individually immobilized to prevent movement. Glass panels must be packed vertically with edge protectors.

**Category C:** Items must be professionally crated in a wooden crate with foam interior lining. Crating must be performed by an Apex Tracking-approved crating service. Temperature-sensitive items must include temperature data loggers. A pre-transit inspection is mandatory.

### 4.3 Prohibited Packaging Materials
The following materials are NOT acceptable for fragile item packaging:
- Newspaper or magazine pages as primary cushioning
- Loose fill packing peanuts for items over 5 lbs
- Single-walled boxes of any kind
- Trash bags, garbage bags, or soft-sided containers
- Used or previously damaged boxes with compromised structural integrity

## 5. Fragile Goods Damaged Goods Policy

### 5.1 Reporting Damage to Fragile Items
Damage to fragile items must be reported using the same process outlined in the Damaged Goods Policy (POL-DMG-002) with the following additional requirements specific to fragile goods:

1. **Immediate Inspection:** Fragile item shipments must be inspected within 2 hours of delivery. Do not sign for delivery until the package has been visually inspected for external damage.
2. **Unpacking Documentation:** The entire unpacking process must be photographed or video recorded, showing the condition of each layer of packaging as it is removed.
3. **Itemized Damage Report:** Each damaged item must be individually photographed and described, including the nature of the damage (crack, chip, complete breakage, etc.).
4. **Packaging Preservation:** All packaging materials, including the outer box, inner cushioning, and individual wrappings, must be preserved in their as-delivered condition for a minimum of 30 days after the claim is filed.

### 5.2 Damage Assessment for Fragile Items
Damage to fragile items is assessed based on the following criteria:

| Damage Type | Definition | Compensation Basis |
|-------------|------------|-------------------|
| Minor Cosmetic | Small chips, scratches, or blemishes that do not affect functionality | 15-25% of declared value |
| Moderate Damage | Cracks, dents, or partial breakage that affects appearance but not core function | 30-50% of declared value |
| Severe Damage | Significant breakage, missing pieces, or structural compromise | 60-80% of declared value |
| Total Loss | Item is completely destroyed, shattered, or irreparable | 100% of declared value (up to insured amount) |

### 5.3 Time Limits for Fragile Claims

| Damage Discovery | Filing Deadline |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Visible damage at delivery | Must be noted on delivery receipt immediately; claim filed within 24 hours |
| Concealed damage discovered during unpacking | Within 48 hours of delivery |
| Latent damage (functional failure discovered after use) | Within 5 business days of delivery |
| Damage to Category C items | Within 12 hours of delivery |

## 6. Refund Procedures for Fragile Items

### 6.1 Refund Eligibility
Refunds for damaged fragile items are subject to the following conditions:

1. The item was properly classified as fragile at the time of booking
2. The packaging met or exceeded the requirements outlined in Section 4
3. The declared value was accurately stated and documented
4. Additional insurance was purchased for items valued over $2,000
5. The damage was not caused by prohibited packaging materials or methods
6. The claim was filed within the applicable time limits

### 6.2 Refund Amounts

| Scenario | Refund Amount | Processing Time |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Proper packaging + insurance purchased | Full declared value up to insured amount | 5 business days |
| Proper packaging + no additional insurance | Up to $2,000 per package (standard liability) | 7 business days |
| Improper packaging by sender | Claim denied — no refund | N/A |
| Category C item with professional crating | Full declared value up to insured amount | 3 business days |
| Category C item without professional crating | Claim denied — liability waiver applies | N/A |

### 6.3 Expedited Refund for Fragile Claims
Fragile item claims valued under $500 may be eligible for expedited processing. Upon submission of clear photographic evidence showing the damage and proper packaging, the claim may be approved within 24 hours without requiring a physical inspection. This expedited process is at the sole discretion of Apex Tracking.

## 7. Insurance Claim Steps for Fragile Items

### 7.1 Step-by-Step Insurance Claim Process

**Step 1: Document the Damage (Immediately Upon Discovery)**
- Take photographs of the outer box showing all six sides
- Photograph the interior packaging before removing the item
- Photograph the damaged item from multiple angles
- If possible, video record the unpacking process
- Preserve all packaging materials in their original condition

**Step 2: File the Initial Claim (Within Required Time Limits)**
- Submit claim online at www.apextracking.com/claims/fragile
- Include the tracking number and "FRAGILE CLAIM" in the subject line
- Upload all photographic evidence
- Provide the original receipt or proof of purchase showing item value
- Include the itemized damage assessment

**Step 3: Claim Review and Assessment**
- A dedicated fragile claims adjuster will be assigned within 24 hours
- The adjuster may request additional photographs or documentation
- For claims over $2,500, a physical inspection may be required
- The adjuster will determine if packaging met the required standards

**Step 4: Resolution and Payment**
- Approved claims are paid within the timeframes specified in Section 6.2
- Payment is made via the original payment method or check
- A detailed claim resolution letter is provided explaining the determination
- For denied claims, the letter includes specific reasons and recommendations

### 7.2 Required Documentation Checklist

| Document | Required For |
|----------|-------------|
| Photographs of outer packaging (all 6 sides) | All claims |
| Photographs of inner packaging and cushioning | All claims |
| Photographs of damaged item(s) from multiple angles | All claims |
| Original receipt, invoice, or proof of purchase | Claims over $100 |
| Itemized list of contents with individual values | Multi-item shipments |
| Video of unpacking process | Claims over $1,000 |
| Professional appraisal or valuation certificate | Claims over $5,000 |
| Police report (if applicable) | Theft or suspicious damage |
| Temperature data logs | Temperature-sensitive items |

### 7.3 Insurance Claim Timeframes

| Claim Value | Review Time | Payment Time (after approval) |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| Under $500 | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| $500 - $2,500 | 3 business days | 5 business days |
| $2,500 - $10,000 | 5 business days | 7 business days |
| Over $10,000 | 10 business days | 10 business days |

## 8. Liability Waiver

### 8.1 When a Liability Waiver Applies
A liability waiver may be required in the following circumstances:

1. **Declined Specialized Packaging:** The sender declines professional crating for Category C items and chooses to package the item themselves.
2. **Declined Additional Insurance:** The sender declines to purchase additional insurance for items valued over $2,000.
3. **Non-Standard Items:** The item does not fit within standard packaging guidelines due to size, shape, or material composition.
4. **Self-Shipment:** The sender packages the item themselves without using Apex Tracking's packaging services.
5. **Used or Pre-Owned Items:** Items that are not in new condition and have pre-existing wear.

### 8.2 Waiver Terms and Conditions
By signing a liability waiver, the sender acknowledges and agrees to the following:

1. **Limited Liability:** Apex Tracking's liability for damage to the fragile item(s) is limited to a maximum of $100 per package, regardless of the declared value or actual value of the contents.
2. **Packaging Responsibility:** The sender assumes full responsibility for the adequacy of the packaging. Apex Tracking is not liable for damage caused by insufficient, improper, or inadequate packaging.
3. **No Insurance Coverage:** The standard $2,000 liability coverage does not apply to items covered by a signed liability waiver. The sender must purchase separate insurance through a third-party provider if they desire additional coverage.
4. **Claim Limitations:** Claims for items covered by a liability waiver are limited to the $100 maximum and are subject to additional scrutiny and documentation requirements.
5. **Irreversibility:** Once signed, the liability waiver cannot be revoked or modified after the shipment has been accepted for transit.

### 8.3 Waiver Exceptions
A liability waiver will NOT be accepted in the following situations, and Apex Tracking reserves the right to refuse shipment:

1. Items valued over $25,000 regardless of packaging
2. Category C items that have not been professionally crated
3. Items that require temperature-controlled transit without proper equipment
4. Hazardous or regulated materials combined with fragile items
5. Shipments where the sender cannot provide a reasonable value estimate

### 8.4 Waiver Signing Process
1. The sender must complete the Fragile Goods Liability Waiver form (Form FRG-WVR-001)
2. The form must be signed electronically or physically before the shipment is accepted
3. A copy of the signed waiver is provided to the sender via email
4. The waiver is recorded in the shipping manifest and associated with the tracking number
5. The waiver remains valid for the duration of the shipment's transit

## 9. Special Handling Procedures

### 9.1 Labeling Requirements
All fragile shipments must display the following labels:
- "FRAGILE" label on all six sides of the box (red or orange, minimum 4" x 4")
- "THIS SIDE UP" arrows on two adjacent sides
- "DO NOT STACK" label for Category C items
- "HANDLE WITH CARE" label for Category B and C items
- Temperature range label for temperature-sensitive items

### 9.2 Handling Instructions for Apex Tracking Personnel
1. Fragile packages must be loaded on top of all other packages in the transport vehicle
2. No other packages may be stacked on top of fragile packages
3. Fragile packages must be handled manually — not thrown, dropped, or kicked
4. Fragile packages must be stored in a dry, temperature-controlled area when awaiting pickup or delivery
5. Fragile packages must be placed in a secure, upright position during transit
6. Any visible damage to a fragile package during transit must be immediately reported to the supervisor

### 9.3 Delivery Requirements
1. Fragile shipments require a signature upon delivery
2. The recipient must be given the opportunity to inspect the package before signing
3. If the recipient refuses to sign, the package will be returned to the local facility for pickup
4. Fragile shipments cannot be left at the door without a signature unless a specific waiver has been signed by the recipient
5. Delivery personnel must note any visible damage on the delivery record

## 10. Exclusions and Limitations

The following are not covered under the Fragile Goods Policy:

1. Damage caused by improper packing by the sender, including failure to meet the minimum packaging standards outlined in Section 4
2. Pre-existing damage, defects, or manufacturing flaws that existed before the shipment was accepted
3. Damage caused by the recipient during the unpacking process
4. Normal wear and tear, including minor scuffs, scratches, or blemishes that do not affect functionality
5. Items that are inherently unable to withstand the normal rigors of transportation, regardless of packaging
6. Damage caused by acts of God, including earthquakes, floods, hurricanes, or extreme temperatures
7. Items that were not declared as fragile at the time of booking
8. Damage to items shipped under a signed liability waiver beyond the $100 limit
9. Consequential damages, including loss of use, lost profits, or emotional distress
10. Items that are prohibited or restricted under Apex Tracking's Terms of Service

## 11. Dispute Resolution for Fragile Claims

### 11.1 Internal Appeal Process
If a fragile goods claim is denied or the settlement amount is disputed, the customer may file an appeal:

1. Submit a written appeal to fragileclaims@apextracking.com within 15 business days of the claim decision
2. Include the claim number, tracking number, and detailed reasons for the appeal
3. Provide any additional evidence or documentation not included in the original claim
4. The appeal will be reviewed by a senior claims adjuster within 5 business days
5. A final written determination will be provided within 10 business days of the appeal submission

### 11.2 Third-Party Mediation
If the internal appeal process does not resolve the dispute, either party may request third-party mediation through the American Arbitration Association (AAA). Mediation costs are shared equally between Apex Tracking and the customer unless otherwise determined by the mediator.

### 11.3 Legal Action
Customers retain the right to pursue legal action for claims exceeding $10,000. Any legal action must be filed within one year of the delivery date or the scheduled delivery date for lost packages. Legal proceedings shall be conducted in the jurisdiction where Apex Tracking's principal place of business is located.
