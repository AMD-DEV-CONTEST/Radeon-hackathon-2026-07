Condition: Anxiety (general lifestyle and dietary support only)

Recommended foods:
Magnesium-rich foods, whole grains, herbal teas (chamomile), foods rich in omega-3.

Healthy habits:
Regular exercise, breathing/relaxation techniques, consistent sleep schedule, professional counseling support.

Avoid:
Excess caffeine, excess alcohol, high-sugar processed foods.

Note: Dietary and lifestyle habits can support wellbeing but are not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. Please consult a licensed mental health professional.
