Condition: PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)

Recommended foods:
High-fiber foods, lean protein, low-glycemic carbohydrates, anti-inflammatory foods (fatty fish, leafy greens).

Healthy habits:
Regular exercise, maintain healthy weight, manage stress, regular checkups.

Avoid:
Refined carbohydrates, sugary drinks, processed foods, excess dairy (may worsen symptoms for some).
