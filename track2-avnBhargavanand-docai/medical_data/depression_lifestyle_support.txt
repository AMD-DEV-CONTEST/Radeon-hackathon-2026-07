Condition: Depression (general lifestyle and dietary support only)

Recommended foods:
Omega-3 rich foods, whole grains, fruits and vegetables, foods rich in folate and B vitamins.

Healthy habits:
Regular physical activity, consistent sleep schedule, social connection, professional therapy/counseling support.

Avoid:
Excess alcohol, excess caffeine, highly processed foods.

Note: Dietary and lifestyle habits can support wellbeing but are not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. Please consult a licensed mental health professional.
