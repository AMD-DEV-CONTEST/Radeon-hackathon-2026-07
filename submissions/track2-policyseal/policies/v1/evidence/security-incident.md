# Synthetic Security Incident Rules

These rules apply only to the PolicySeal competition incident-response fixture.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-DOCX-001 -->
## Incident investigation facts

The external response partner receives the event timeline, malicious external indicators,
relevant log clues, vulnerability facts, investigation roles, and remediation status needed to
assist the investigation.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-DOCX-002 -->
## Stable aliases for internal identities

Internal hosts, addresses, employees, accounts, and remediation owners use stable aliases within
one task. A different task uses a different mapping so identities cannot be linked through the
alias alone.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-DOCX-003 -->
## Credentials and exploit material

Passwords, tokens, private keys, complete exploit commands, payloads, and attack scripts are
removed. Residual credential risk blocks the revision and cannot be waived by approval.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-DOCX-004 -->
## Internal management information

Personal contact details, internal blame, performance judgments, remediation budgets, and
management comments are not necessary for the response partner's technical investigation.
