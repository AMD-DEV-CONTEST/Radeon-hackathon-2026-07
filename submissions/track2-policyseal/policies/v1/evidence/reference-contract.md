# Synthetic Reference Contract Rules

These rules apply only to the PolicySeal competition reference-contract fixture.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-PDF-001 -->
## Reference draft placeholders

The candidate supplier receives a document titled Reference Draft - Not for Signature. Original
parties, corporate identifiers, contacts, absolute contract dates, and historical pricing are
replaced with fixed semantic placeholders. Bank accounts are removed.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-PDF-002 -->
## Standard contract terms

Approved standard legal language, standard payment periods, and relative performance periods may
remain because they are needed to discuss the proposed terms without identifying the old deal.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-PDF-003 -->
## Nonstandard obligations need approval

Custom service levels, special indemnities, and other nonstandard obligations require approval of
the exact immutable revision, or replacement with a safer approved placeholder in a new revision.

<!-- evidence-id: EV-PDF-004 -->
## Signature and source metadata exclusion

Signatures, seals, signing identities, handwritten content, PDF annotations, author fields, and
source file paths are not copied into the generated DOCX reference draft.
