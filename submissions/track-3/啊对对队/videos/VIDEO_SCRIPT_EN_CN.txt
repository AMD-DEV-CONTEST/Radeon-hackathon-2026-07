================================================================

Purpose / 用途
--------------
This is an editing script, not a record of a new experiment. The supplied robot
clips are eight successful Genesis simulation episodes produced by
ScriptedPickPlaceExpert + ClosedLoopSupervisor (8/10 fixed seeds). They are
reference/expert trajectories, not VLA success videos. Add Chinese narration
and the paired English subtitle below. Keep claims on screen as written.

这是剪辑脚本，不是新的实验记录。随包机械臂片段来自 Genesis 仿真中
ScriptedPickPlaceExpert + ClosedLoopSupervisor 的 8 个成功回合（10 个固定种子中 8 个成功）。
它们是脚本专家/参考轨迹，不是 VLA 成功视频。中文配音配对应英文字幕即可，画面中的结论请按本稿表述。

Editing rules / 剪辑约束
------------------------
- Deliver at 1920x1080 or higher. The supplied robot material is 224x224; it
  may be enlarged with a neutral background or shown inside a clean layout,
  but do not imply it was originally 1080p.
- The first five seconds must show a close, readable grasp moment. Crop or
  enlarge only for presentation; retain the source provenance in the caption.
- Retain all eight source episodes somewhere in the 0:30-1:30 demonstration
  section. The raw 45.6-second concatenation may remain uncut as the backbone.
- Add bilingual subtitles. Avoid simulated sensor overlays, invented success
  counters, made-up real-robot footage, or claims of deployment on hardware.
- End with the public GitHub URL and a real team contact supplied by the
  submitter. Replace all square-bracket placeholders before upload.
