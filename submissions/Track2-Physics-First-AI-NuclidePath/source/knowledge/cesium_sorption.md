# Cesium-137 sorption and potassium competition

Cesium sorption in soils is strongly controlled by mineralogy. Illitic clay minerals contain frayed-edge sites with high selectivity for Cs+, while competing cations such as K+ can affect access to and occupancy of selective sites. The magnitude is system-specific and depends on clay content, weathering, ionic strength and concentration history.

For this project, `Kd = 0.2 m³/kg` is classified as a **low-end literature screening value**, not a universal property. The empirical coefficient used to reduce `Kd` as K+ increases is classified as **demonstrative** until calibrated against site or experimental data.
