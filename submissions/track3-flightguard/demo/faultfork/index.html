03 · WHY FLIGHTGUARD

From visible failure to a controller decision.

FlightGuard connects measured capability, Radeon execution, and a practical pre-hardware workflow.

CAPABILITY

Matched flight outcomes

Primary success rises from 194/384 to 371/384; retention rises from 121/192 to 192/192 with zero nominal-only wins.

RADEON EXECUTION

One GPU, batched worlds

One visible Radeon processes 2,227,200 transitions and scales from 4,632.58 to 74,246.46 transitions/s across 32→512 environments.

APPLICATION VALUE

Qualify before hardware

Researchers can expose a failure, test one controller change across multiple seeds, and decide what deserves hardware-test time.

04 · ONE-RADEON EXECUTION

GPU scaling belongs to the evidence path.

The fixed single-GPU workload scales within one Radeon from 32 to 512 environments. Throughput is kept separate from controller science.
