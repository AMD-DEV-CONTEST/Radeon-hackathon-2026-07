# Qwen3.6 teacher swap trial (July 28) — Gate 3 FAILED, no swap; A/B is submission gold

One-liner: gated trial of Qwen3.6-35B-A3B as teacher on the gfx1100:
speed and JSON gates passed, but the decisive external-ground-truth
gate failed (its student: 0.92 exact vs incumbent's 0.95 on the fixed
policy-labeled holdout; 98.9% vs 100% on unambiguous) — so the
submission keeps Qwen3-30B-A3B, and the trial itself becomes measured
evidence for the report: the factory A/B'd two teacher generations in
one afternoon.

Gate results (trial runs slice-20260728-153449 pilot, -164336 full):
1. Speed: PASS-ish — tg 84.1 tok/s (vs incumbent 110.2; ≥60 bar), pp
   1944 (vs 1400). BUT full-scale datagen 2544s = 42.4 min vs 17.6 —
   2.4× slower at production context lengths (GDN kernel gap real).
2. JSON: PASS — pilot 97.2% acceptance, clean samples. Full run: 96.2%
   vs incumbent's 99.0-99.2%.
3. External ground truth: **FAIL** — 3.6-trained student vs fixed
   policy-truth holdout: 0.92 exact (cat 0.94 / pri 0.93), unambiguous
   0.989 (91/92). Incumbent student: 0.95 / 1.00. Bar was ≥0.95.
4-5. Skipped per the pre-agreed abort rule.

Two findings worth quoting:
- **Self-agreement inflates**: the 3.6 student scored 0.98 on its own
  teacher's holdout but 0.92 on external truth — exactly why the
  policy-true audit exists. (Incumbent: 0.97 self, 0.95 external —
  much smaller gap.)
- **Benchmark rank ≠ labeling-transfer quality**: 3.6 is far above
  the 30B on GPQA/AIME, yet its labels transferred policy *worse*,
  especially on ambiguous dual-issue tickets where its dominant-issue
  instincts differ from the policy reading.

Honest caveat carried into the report: the fixed holdout's tickets
were generated by the 30B teacher, so the incumbent student has a mild
style-familiarity advantage on it. Doesn't flip the decision — speed
(2.4×) and acceptance also favored the incumbent — but stated.
