# ChronoSafe-Drive Dataset Card

## Dataset sources

ChronoSafe-Drive uses two independently generated sources:

1. **MetaDrive simulation data** generated by `scripts/collect_dataset.py`.
2. **nuScenes v1.0-mini or v1.0-trainval sensor data** converted locally by
   `scripts/build_nuscenes_degradation_dataset.py`.

The repository does not redistribute raw nuScenes images, point clouds or
archives. Users must obtain nuScenes from its official website and comply with
the current official terms, attribution requirements and usage restrictions.

## Modalities

- MetaDrive offscreen RGB camera and 64-ray LiDAR.
- nuScenes `CAM_FRONT`, `LIDAR_TOP` and ego-pose timestamps.
- Ten causal sensor-health features; no clean reference is supplied to the
  diagnosis model at inference time.
- The timing-aware research variant adds observable packet age, inter-sensor
  timestamp skew, cross-modal change disagreement and LiDAR zero-ratio change.
  These values come from received sensor packets and causal history, not from a
  clean reference stream.

## Real-data synchronization

All `CAM_FRONT` sample and sweep records are associated with their scene and
paired to the temporally nearest `LIDAR_TOP` record. Pairs beyond the configured
maximum synchronization gap are discarded. Ego speed is estimated causally
from timestamped ego translations. LiDAR point clouds are converted into 64
azimuthal minimum-range bins so their representation matches the simulator's
fixed-size range stream.

## Degradation labels

The seven controlled conditions are:

1. clean;
2. progressive camera blur;
3. camera freeze;
4. LiDAR dropout;
5. dynamic cross-modal delay;
6. camera freeze plus LiDAR dropout;
7. camera blur plus dynamic delay.

Labels describe programmatically injected sensor degradation, not native
nuScenes annotations. Risk targets are continuous functions of blur severity,
freeze state, dropout ratio and delay length. Small photometric and range noise
augmentations are applied to reduce duplicate tracks.

## Splits and leakage prevention

- Simulation train/calibration/test partitions are separated by MetaDrive seed.
- Trainval real-data partitions are grouped by complete nuScenes log, so
  adjacent scenes from the same collection drive cannot cross partitions.
- Every augmented track from one real scene remains in the same partition.
- Risk calibration uses only calibration domains; test domains remain unseen.

## Intended use

The generated features support research and demonstration of temporal sensor
health diagnosis, calibrated risk prediction and minimal-risk control. They are
not a certified safety dataset and must not be used as the sole basis for
safety-critical deployment.

## Known limitations

- nuScenes mini contains only ten scenes and is used only to validate the
  pipeline; the competition-scale experiment uses all 850 trainval scenes.
- Injected degradations approximate sensor faults and do not cover every
  hardware, weather or communication failure.
- Closed-loop control is evaluated in MetaDrive; nuScenes evaluation is
  offline because nuScenes is a logged dataset.
- Dataset terms must be rechecked before publishing derived files.
