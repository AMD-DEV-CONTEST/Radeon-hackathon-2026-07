# Final submission fallback plan

## Purpose

The final official Radeon tunnel retry ended at the DNS gate without loading
the model. The submission will therefore demonstrate the complete local
product honestly and connect it to previously verified Radeon evidence. It
will not portray mock output as GPU output or claim a completed public browser
E2E.

## Submission narrative

1. BrandPilot Local solves campaign setup, multimodal asset handling, creative
   exploration, and human review in a private local-first workspace.
2. The polished responsive application is complete in explicit mock mode.
3. Separate controlled runs verified SDXL image-to-image inference on AMD
   Radeon through ROCm and authenticated FastAPI inference on cloud loopback.
4. The official Radeon `rc-tunnel` installer, identity, and activation were
   verified, but the assigned hostname repeatedly lacked public DNS.
5. The team preserved TLS and platform security, escalated through the official
   Luma host channel, and declined unofficial tunnels or network overrides.
6. The next engineering milestone is product-preserving generation after an
   approved public transport is available.

## Allowed claims

- BrandPilot Local is a multimodal campaign creation application.
- The responsive frontend and FastAPI backend work locally.
- Mock mode is complete, default, and clearly disclosed.
- Standalone SDXL image-to-image inference ran on AMD Radeon through ROCm.
- Authenticated FastAPI loopback inference ran on Radeon.
- Official `rc-tunnel` installation and activation were verified.
- The public browser edge was blocked by the official hostname DNS failure.
- The blocker was escalated privately through Luma without a response before
  the final bounded retry.
- The application includes human review and persistent project/result state.

## Prohibited claims

- Completed browser-to-Radeon generation E2E.
- Production deployment or permanent public hosting.
- Continuous availability of a live Radeon backend.
- IP-Adapter, masking, or exact product-identity preservation.
- Perfect packaging text or logo preservation.
- Commercial or model-license clearance.
- Final performance optimization.

## Assets and evidence

Use only the premium frontend screenshots, rights-safe examples, Session 1/2
Radeon evidence, authenticated loopback evidence, Session 3 sanitized DNS
reports, and the cropped destroyed-instance proof. Never include a private
hostname, token, instance/account identifier, browser profile, client asset,
cache, weight, or raw terminal screenshot.
