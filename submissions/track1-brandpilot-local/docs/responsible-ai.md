# Responsible AI

Every generated campaign requires human review. Users must have rights to input assets and must verify copyright, trademarks, brand permissions, factual claims, product-safety claims, and disclosure obligations before publication.

The product must avoid demographic stereotyping, deceptive edits, impersonation, dangerous or unlawful promotion, misleading performance or health claims, and unauthorized brand use. Generated media must remain disclosed wherever context could otherwise mislead.

Private-client assets must stay out of version control and should receive explicit retention and deletion controls before production use. Users remain responsible for their inputs and final publication decisions. A production policy will define rejected content and appeal behavior.

Models can reproduce bias, visual artifacts, inaccurate text, and training-data patterns. Human review, provenance records, conservative defaults, and clear limitations are required even after real inference is connected.
