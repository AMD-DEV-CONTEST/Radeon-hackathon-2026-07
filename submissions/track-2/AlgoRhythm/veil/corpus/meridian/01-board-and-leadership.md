## Sites

Meridian operates four sites: Bristol (headquarters, 1,840 staff), Exeter
(1,120 staff), Taunton (760 staff), and Yeovil (480 staff). Total headcount as
of the 2024 annual report is approximately 4,200.

## Directorate structure

The Governance and Risk directorate comprises 34 staff across the four sites,
of whom 6 are based at Bristol. The directorate has had one director since its
creation in 2019.
