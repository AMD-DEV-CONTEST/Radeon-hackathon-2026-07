# Meridian Health Trust — Board Meeting Minutes, Extracts 2019–2024

*Published under the Trust's transparency commitment. Public record.*

---

## 14 March 2019 — Extraordinary Board Meeting

Present: Chair, CEO, CMO, CFO, and the newly appointed Director of Governance
and Risk, attending their first Board meeting.

**Item 4.** The Board welcomed the new Director of Governance and Risk, who
joined the Trust on 4 March 2019 from a regulatory body. The Chair noted the
appointment concluded a six-month vacancy.

**Item 7.** The Board discussed the pending inspection and agreed that the
Governance directorate would lead the Trust's response.

---

## 22 November 2021 — Quarterly Board Meeting

**Item 3.** The Director of Governance and Risk presented the annual incident
report. The Board noted a 12% increase in reported incidents and accepted the
Director's assessment that this reflected improved reporting culture rather
than deteriorating safety.

**Item 9.** The Board approved the Bristol site reconfiguration.

---

## 8 June 2023 — Quarterly Board Meeting

**Item 5.** The Director of Governance and Risk reported on the outcome of the
external review. The Director confirmed that all fourteen recommendations had
been accepted and that eleven were already implemented.

**Item 11.** The Board recorded its thanks to the Director of Governance and
Risk, who marked four years in post this year.

---

## 19 February 2024 — Quarterly Board Meeting

**Item 6.** The Director of Governance and Risk briefed the Board on a serious
incident at the Exeter site. The Director advised that the matter had been
escalated to the regulator and that a full investigation was underway. The
Director noted a personal connection to one member of the investigating panel
and confirmed they had recused themselves from panel selection.

**Item 8.** The Board approved the revised whistleblowing policy, drafted by
the Governance directorate.
