# Meridian Health Trust — Press Coverage and Public Filings

*Third-party material. Public record.*

## Bristol Evening Chronicle — 6 March 2019

**Trust appoints governance chief after six-month gap**

Meridian Health Trust has appointed a new Director of Governance and Risk,
ending a vacancy that opened when the previous postholder left in September
2018. The appointee joins from a national regulatory body, where they led
inspection policy for four years.

The Trust said the new director would be based at its Bristol headquarters and
would take responsibility for incident reporting and regulatory compliance.

---

## Health Service Weekly — 14 September 2021

**Meridian incident reporting up 12%, director says that's the point**

Meridian's governance director told a conference in Manchester that a rise in
reported incidents at the Trust reflected staff confidence rather than falling
standards. "If your incident numbers are going down, either you have solved
patient safety or people have stopped telling you things," they said.

The director, who joined Meridian in 2019 and previously worked in regulation,
has overseen a restructure of the Trust's reporting system.

## Bristol Evening Chronicle — 3 March 2024

**Meridian faces questions over Exeter incident**

Meridian Health Trust has referred a serious incident at its Exeter site to the
regulator. The Trust's governance director, who has held the post for five
years, confirmed an investigation was underway but declined to comment further.

A Trust spokesperson said the director had recused themselves from selecting
the investigating panel owing to a personal connection.
