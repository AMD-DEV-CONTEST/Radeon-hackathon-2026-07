This is an adapted, paraphrased, and anonymized public-research fixture, not
first-party testimony.

I spent about two weeks trying a high-end mesh office chair because I sit at a
desk most of the day and wanted something cool and visually clean in a small
home office. The mesh breathed well, but the front edge felt hard for me and I
kept shifting during long days.

Because the store had a return window, I exchanged it for a padded ergonomic
chair with more adjustments. After roughly another two weeks, the padding and
support fit me better and I stopped fidgeting. My takeaway was that chair fit is
individual and a real trial with a return policy mattered more than hype.
