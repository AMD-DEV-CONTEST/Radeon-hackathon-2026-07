# Company Leave Policy (Sample Private Document)

## Scope
This document applies to full-time employees of DemoTech Co.

## Annual Leave
- Employees receive **10 days** of paid annual leave after probation.
- After 3 years of service, annual leave becomes **15 days**.
- Leave requests must be submitted in the HR system **at least 3 working days** in advance.

## Sick Leave
- Paid sick leave requires a medical certificate for absences longer than **1 day**.
- Maximum paid sick leave per year: **15 days**.

## Remote Work
- Remote work is allowed up to **2 days per week** with manager approval.
- Sensitive customer data must stay inside the private knowledge workspace and must not be uploaded to public SaaS tools.

## Approvals
- Leave <= 3 days: direct manager approval.
- Leave > 3 days: manager + HR approval.
