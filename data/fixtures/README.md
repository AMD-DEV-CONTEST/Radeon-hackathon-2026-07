# XiangLens Image Fixture Pack

This directory contains a small, reproducible image dataset for the XiangLens demo and evaluation workflow.

## Composition

- 16 existing portrait photographs from Wikimedia Summit 2019;
- 8 existing open cultural images covering bat, lotus, dragon, and bamboo motifs;
- 1 public-domain QR image used only as a deterministic overlay;
- 120 derived test fixtures at 512 by 512 pixels.

No image in this pack was generated by an AI image model.

The 120 fixtures consist of:

- 80 portrait fixtures: clean, tight crop, small subject, low contrast, and one rotating challenge variant;
- 40 cultural fixtures: clean, circular preview, small subject, low contrast, and busy background.

Rotating portrait challenges cover QR codes, fake badges, fake screen text, fake location signs, small text, multiple subjects, GPS EXIF, and device EXIF. All inserted names, identifiers, addresses, email domains, tokens, and timestamps are fictional test values.

## Files

```text
data/fixtures/
├── source_catalog.yaml
├── source_manifest.yaml
├── manifest.yaml
├── LICENSE.images
├── sources/
└── images/
```

`source_catalog.yaml` is the short human-maintained selection list. `source_manifest.yaml` and `manifest.yaml` are generated and include source pages, authors, licenses, and file hashes.

## Build

The builder requires Python 3, Pillow, Requests, and PyYAML.

```bash
python3 scripts/build_image_fixtures.py
```

Use `--force` to download the registered Commons sources again.

## Ethical Boundary

Portrait files depict real Wikimedia event participants and are used under their listed Creative Commons licenses. XiangLens may evaluate crop behavior, small-size clarity, visible privacy risks, and platform fit. It must not use these files for face recognition, identity matching, protected-attribute classification, personality inference, employability scoring, or other sensitive inference.

The cultural images document particular artworks or decorative contexts. They do not establish a universal modern interpretation of a motif.
