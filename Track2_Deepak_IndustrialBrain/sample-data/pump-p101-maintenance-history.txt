APEX PETROCHEM PLANT
Maintenance History: Centrifugal Pump P-101
Location: Boiler Feed Section A

12-Jan-2025
Operator Ravi Kumar reported high vibration of 6.8 mm/s and bearing temperature of 92 C.
The maintenance team inspected Pump P-101, lubricated the drive-end bearing, and tightened the base bolts.
The pump returned to service after vibration reduced to 4.2 mm/s.

18-Mar-2025
Pump P-101 tripped after bearing temperature reached 108 C.
Inspection found bearing wear and dark, contaminated lubricant. The drive-end bearing was replaced.
The work order recommended an oil contamination investigation and shaft alignment check.

04-Jul-2025
High vibration returned at 7.6 mm/s. Laser alignment found 0.42 mm angular misalignment.
The motor and pump were aligned and the coupling insert was replaced.

22-Nov-2025
Pump P-101 was stopped after vibration reached 8.2 mm/s and discharge pressure fell from 15 bar to 12 bar.
The bearing showed repeated wear. Lubrication contamination and delayed seal replacement were recorded.
The team replaced the bearing and mechanical seal, flushed the bearing housing, and tested the pump.

Maintenance conclusion:
Pump P-101 experienced three significant bearing-related events during 2025.
The recurring pattern links bearing wear with lubricant contamination, misalignment, high vibration, and high temperature.
