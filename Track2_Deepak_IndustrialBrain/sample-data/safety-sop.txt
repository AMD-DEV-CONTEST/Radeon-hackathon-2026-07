Safety SOP: Rotating Equipment Maintenance

Before opening Pump P-101 or any rotating equipment, personnel must:
1. Obtain an approved work permit.
2. Stop the equipment using the normal shutdown sequence.
3. Isolate electrical power and apply lockout-tagout.
4. Close process isolation valves and release stored pressure.
5. Verify zero energy before beginning work.
6. Wear the specified PPE and record the maintenance activity.
