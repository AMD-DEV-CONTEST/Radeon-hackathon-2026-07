OEM OPERATING AND MAINTENANCE GUIDE
Asset family: P-100 series centrifugal process pump
Applicable equipment tag: P-101

Normal operating limits:
- Discharge pressure: 14 to 16 bar
- Bearing temperature: below 90 C
- Overall vibration: below 6.0 mm/s
- Speed: 2,950 rpm

Troubleshooting:
High vibration may be associated with shaft misalignment, bearing damage, cavitation, loose foundations, or coupling defects.
Repeated bearing failure requires verification of lubricant cleanliness, lubrication interval, alignment, shaft runout, and operating load.
Low discharge pressure may be associated with cavitation, impeller damage, suction restriction, or internal wear.

Safety:
Isolate electrical energy, close suction and discharge valves, release pressure, and apply lockout-tagout before maintenance.
