### Project: RadTrot — Robust Quadruped Locomotion on AMD Radeon GPUs

**Track:** 3 (Physical AI)
**Team:** boost_

**Summary:**
A Unitree Go1 quadruped (simulated in MuJoCo) walks with a trot gait
generated by a Central Pattern Generator, stress-tested against randomized
terrain friction and random external pushes to measure locomotion
robustness. A lightweight CNN terrain classifier is benchmarked on AMD
Radeon GPU (ROCm) vs. CPU to demonstrate real-time inference feasibility
for future closed-loop terrain-adaptive gait control.

**Key Results:**
- Robot survived 4 random pushes on both terrains without falling
- Easy terrain: 1.00 m traveled, max tilt 0.044 rad
- Hard terrain: 1.15 m traveled, max tilt 0.040 rad
- AMD Radeon GPU (ROCm): 0.212 ms/batch — **11.29× faster than CPU**
