This is AutoQuant, a private quantitative investment assistant built for China's domestic securities market. It is more than a chatbot, and more than a code generator. Users describe an investment idea in natural language. AutoQuant turns it into a strategy that can be validated, backtested, audited, and independently reviewed by a risk agent. Model fine-tuning and inference run locally on AMD Radeon GPU hardware with ROCm, without depending on a hosted language-model API.
