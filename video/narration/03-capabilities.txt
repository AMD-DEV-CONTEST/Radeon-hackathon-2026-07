AutoQuant demonstrates all five agent capabilities required by the competition. Reasoning uses a ReAct loop of thought, action, and observation. Planning routes each request to conversational assistance, local computation, information retrieval, or strategy generation. Tools cover knowledge retrieval, indicator calculation, strategy validation, backtesting, and reporting. Memory is divided into working, episodic, and semantic layers. Task execution completes the entire workflow, from an initial requirement to a reproducible risk report.
