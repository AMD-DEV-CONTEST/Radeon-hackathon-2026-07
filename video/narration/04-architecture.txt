The complete workflow has eight stages. Input first passes through intent routing and retrieval-augmented generation. A LoRA-adapted Qwen 2.5 seven-billion-parameter model then generates the strategy. Deterministic code handles canonicalization, validation, simulated backtesting, and risk calculation, producing a final verdict of pass, review, or reject. Dify and Open WebUI both call the same vLLM service through an OpenAI-compatible interface. The user interface can therefore change while the AMD-local model, tools, and safety boundaries remain consistent.
