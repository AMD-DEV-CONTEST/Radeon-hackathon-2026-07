This dashboard shows the product's core closed loop. On the left is a natural-language request for a CSI 300 moving-average strategy. On the right is the generated structured strategy. The system never trusts model text directly. It applies type correction, domestic-market constraint completion, and semantic validation. The backtest layer explicitly models T-plus-one settlement, board-lot trading, the ban on naked short selling, price limits, fees, and slippage. The final report presents return, drawdown, Sharpe ratio, and an independent risk verdict.
