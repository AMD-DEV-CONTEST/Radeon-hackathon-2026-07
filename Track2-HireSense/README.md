## Project Overview

HireSense is an AI-powered Resume and Job Matching Platform that helps job seekers improve their chances of getting hired. The platform analyzes resumes, compares them with job descriptions, identifies missing skills, calculates ATS compatibility scores, and provides personalized recommendations to improve employability.

The system simplifies resume evaluation by providing intelligent insights into resume quality, skill gaps, and job compatibility, enabling users to prepare stronger job applications.

By providing AI-powered recommendations and ATS analysis, the platform supports better career preparation and improves the overall job application process.
